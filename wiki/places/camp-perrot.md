## Overview

Camp Perrot was a boys' camp operated by the Montreal YMCA alongside Kamp Kanawana.^1 It has its own dedicated archival sub-series (P0145/12G) within the Concordia University Archives YMCA of Montreal fonds.^2 The camp's name suggests a location on or near Île Perrot, approximately 40 km west of Montreal, though no archival source explicitly confirms the location.

## Documentary Evidence

The earliest known documentary evidence is a "Comparison of Two Boys Camps operated by Montreal YMCA" dated approximately 1945–1946, which formally compared Camp Perrot and Kamp Kanawana.^1 ^3 A second comparison document dates from 1953–1954.^3 The YMCA produced these comparison reports as part of its camping program evaluation, indicating Camp Perrot was considered a peer facility to Kanawana.

Camp Perrot is absent from the 1980 YMCA situation reports, which mention Kanawana, Otoreke, and Weredale but not Perrot, suggesting it had closed by that date.^1

## Connection to Kanawana

Île Perrot was also a canoe trip destination for Kanawana campers: by 1945, intermediate campers undertook 7–8 day trips to Île Perrot and Grenville.^4 Whether Camp Perrot served as a waypoint or base for these canoe trips, or was an entirely separate residential camp, is unknown.

R.H. Hanagan, who served as Montreal YMCA camp director in 1942 and 1946, filed reports that appear alongside Camp Perrot comparison documents in the Concordia Archives, indicating his portfolio may have extended to both camps.^1
