# Camp Becsies

*Status: stub | Sources: 3*
*Last Updated: 2026-06-22*

## Overview

Camp Becsies was a YMCA of Montreal camp located on Lac des Becs-scie in the Saint-Sauveur area of the Laurentians, in the same municipality as Camp Kanawana. The camp had two distinct operational periods: an initial phase from 1929 to 1936 during which it served youth campers including children from the Montreal Protestant Orphans' Home, and a later phase from 1960 to 1971 when it was repurposed as a family camping site. Archival records for Camp Becsies are held in Concordia University Archives as sub-series P0145/12D, comprising 3 cm of textual records.

## Location

Camp Becsies was situated on Lac des Becs-scie, a lake straddling Saint-Sauveur (approximately 83% of the lake area) and Mille-Isles (approximately 17%). The lake has a total area of 0.5 km², is approximately 2.2 km long and 155-340 m wide.^1 Its location in the same municipality as Camp Kanawana suggests a close geographic and institutional relationship. Lac des Becs-scie may be one of the "three private lakes" historically associated with the Kanawana property, though this connection is inferred rather than confirmed.^1

## First Period: Youth Camp (1929-1936)

The earliest records for Camp Becsies date to 1929.^2 During this period, the camp served youth campers including children from the Montreal Protestant Orphans' Home, documented in 1934 and 1936.^3 The nature of the programming, staffing, and relationship to Camp Kanawana during this period is not documented in available online sources.

## Second Period: Family Camping (1960-1971)

Camp Becsies was repurposed for family camping in its second operational period. A family camping brochure from c. 1962 and administrative records spanning 1960-1971 survive in the Concordia Archives.^3 Photographs of Camp Becsies also exist in the archives.^3

## Archival Records

The camp's records are held at Concordia University Archives as sub-series P0145/12D within the YMCA of Montreal fonds. The collection comprises 3 cm of textual records spanning both operational periods (1929-1936 and 1960-1971).^2 Camp Becsies is one of several YMCA Montreal camps documented in the fonds alongside Camp Kanawana (12B), Camp Otoreke (12C), Camp Dorval (12E), Camp Weredale (12F), and Camp Perrot (12G).^2
