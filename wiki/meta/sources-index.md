## Summary

This article catalogs all sources consulted for the Kanawana Wiki Builder project. As of February 2026, the knowledge base draws on 42 source records spanning primary camp publications (1921-1980), archival finding aids, academic research, and web references. The collection is anchored by Grace McMorris's 2023 Concordia MA thesis, six Internet Archive primary documents, and Concordia University's YMCA of Montreal Fonds P145.
