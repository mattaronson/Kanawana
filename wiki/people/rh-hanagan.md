# R.H. Hanagan

## Overview

R.H. Hanagan served as Montreal YMCA camp director in 1942 and 1946, a period that included managing the camp during wartime conditions and handling a polio outbreak. He is one of the best-documented directors of the 1940s era thanks to his detailed season reports preserved in the Concordia Archives and a 1942 Montreal Gazette article.

## Camp Directorship

Hanagan held the title of "Montreal YMCA camp director" rather than simply "camp director," suggesting he had broader responsibilities across YMCA camping operations.^1 He wrote detailed season reports that survive in the Concordia University Archives.^1

### 1942 Season

A Montreal Gazette article from August 5, 1942, titled "Y.M.C.A. Boys Stage Parade and Circus," describes a camp event with Hanagan "in Indian garb on horseback."^3 The article also names E.E. Smee as "Resident Director," documenting a two-tier directorship structure during wartime where Smee handled day-to-day operations while Hanagan served as overall camp director.^3 The 1942 camp organized sections as Bantam, Junior, Intermediate, and Senior.^3

### 1946 Season and Polio Outbreak

Hanagan managed the 1946 polio outbreak at Camp Kanawana, in which six cases were reported.^1 His detailed report on the outbreak is held in the Concordia Archives.^1
