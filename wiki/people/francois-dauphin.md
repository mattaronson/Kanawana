# François Dauphin

*Status: stub | Sources: 2*

## Overview

François Dauphin held a head-office administrative title, "Directeur YMCA Kanawana," in the Montreal YMCA's 2007 Senior Management roster — a previously undocumented director, discovered only in a direct 2026-07 read of the archived annual report. His documented tenure is brief and administratively ambiguous.

## Documented Role (2007)

The Montreal YMCA's 2007 Annual Report lists, in its Senior Management ("Cadres supérieurs") roster: "Dauphin, François — Directeur YMCA Kanawana."^1 By the 2008 report, he had moved to "Directeur, Centre Y Guy-Favreau," a different YMCA branch.^2 This single-year appearance is read as a head-office administrative designation held concurrently with Sean Day's on-site directorship that year, rather than a separate on-site directorship — see [[people/directors-index|Directors and Staff of Camp Kanawana]]'s Two-Tier Era discussion for the fuller reasoning and the open structural question this raises about how the annual reports' single "Director" listings map onto the Executive/on-site split.
