# Nicolas Garcia

*Status: draft | Sources: 3*
*Last Updated: 2026-07-11*

## Overview

Nicolas Garcia served as Kanawana's on-site Director under Executive Director Sean Day, documented continuously from June 2009 through March 2014. His name was long recorded in this KB as "Nicholas Garcia" from oral history before a direct archival read confirmed the "Nicolas" spelling.

## Directorship (2009–2014)

A Camp YMCA Kanawana 2009 French-language Parent Guide closes with a welcome-letter signature block reading "Sean Day / Nicolas Garcia / Roxanne Martel — Directeur du Camp / Chef de Camp / Coordonnateur administratif," the first primary-source confirmation of Garcia holding the on-site "Chef de Camp" role, and of the correct spelling of his name (without an "h").^1 The camp's official "Contact Us" page, archived at multiple Wayback Machine snapshots between June 2010 and March 2014, consistently lists Garcia under Sean Day: titled "Chef de Camp" in the 2010–2011 snapshots, then "Coordonnateur du camp de vacances" from 2012 through March 2014 — the same person and role under a changed title.^2 By the next available snapshot, in April 2015, Garcia had been replaced in the role by Kristen Whitelaw.^2

Oral history originally recorded Garcia's tenure as spanning 2005 to 2012. Per the operator's standing policy that documented sources take precedence over oral history where the two directly conflict, this article follows the narrower, independently corroborated June 2009 – March 2014 window; the earlier 2005 start date is not directly contradicted by any document and remains an open, unconfirmed possibility rather than a resolved fact.^3 See [[people/directors-index|Directors and Staff of Camp Kanawana]]'s Revision History for the full resolution record (conflict c_016).
