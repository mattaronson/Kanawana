## Summary

The Cushing family had a multi-generational connection to the YMCA of Montreal and its camping programs. Lemuel Cushing Jr. served as YMCA president in 1869-1870.^1 A later Lemuel Cushing brought the first group of boys to Lake Saint-Joseph in 1892, two years before the formal establishment of Camp Jubilee.^2 W.E. Cushing wrote "Historical sketches—Lake St. Joseph" in 1943, now preserved in the Concordia Archives.^3 A Dr. Cushing advised keeping Camp Kanawana open during the 1946 polio outbreak.^4

## Known Facts

### Lemuel Cushing Sr. (1806-1875)
- Businessman and pioneer in the Ottawa valley^5
- Born in Trois-Rivières, Lower Canada^5
- Married Catherine Hutchins of Lachute on August 25, 1836^5
- Thirteen children, including eight sons active in business^5

### Lemuel Cushing Jr. (1842-1881)
- Montreal lawyer^1
- President of the Montreal YMCA, 1869-1870^1
- Married Ellen Myra Macdougall in 1869^1
- Died 1881 (before the 1892 camping trip)^1

### Lemuel Cushing (1892 camp trip)
- Brought the first group of boys to Lake Saint-Joseph in 1892^2
- This was two years before Billy Ball's formal establishment of Camp Jubilee in 1894^2
- Must be a grandson or later relative of Lemuel Jr. (who died 1881)

### W.E. Cushing
- Wrote "Historical sketches—Lake St. Joseph" in 1943^3
- Held in Concordia Archives, fonds P145/12A^3

### Dr. Cushing
- Advised keeping Camp Kanawana open during the 1946 polio outbreak (six cases; trips cancelled but camp stayed open)^4

### Other Cushings in YMCA Records
- Concordia archives hold research reports mentioning "Lemuel Cushing" and "Charles Cushing" among YMCA figures^3
