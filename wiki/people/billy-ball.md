## Summary

"Billy" Ball of the YMCA of Montreal led the first organized camping trip in 1894, taking a group of boys to an island on Lake Saint-Joseph in the Township of Howard (now Saint-Adolphe-d'Howard), near Sainte-Agathe, Quebec. This trip formally established Camp Jubilee, Quebec's first residential summer camp, with an initial enrollment of 20 campers.^1 ^2

## Known Facts

- Led the first YMCA of Montreal camping trip in summer 1894^1
- Took boys to an island on Lake Saint-Joseph, Township of Howard^1
- The camp was named Camp Jubilee, commemorating the YMCA's 50th anniversary (founded 1844)^3
- First enrollment: 20 campers^2
- Camp Jubilee later moved to Saint-Sauveur-des-Monts (~1909-1910) and was renamed Camp Kanawana^1
