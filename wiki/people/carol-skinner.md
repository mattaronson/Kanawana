# Carol Skinner

*Status: stub | Sources: 2*
*Last Updated: 2026-07-11*

## Overview

Carol Sharman Skinner served as a counsellor and section director at Camp Kanawana from 1990 to 1995, and went on to a career in camp administration and art therapy. Diagnosed with ALS in 2013, she became a public advocate for ALS Canada. She received the Pip Alumni Award in 2016, presented before campers on July 7 and again at the September alumni gathering.^1 ^2

## Camp Kanawana (1990–1995)

Skinner worked as a counsellor and section director — not overall camp director — at Kanawana across these five seasons.^1

## Later Career

After Kanawana, Skinner became Director of YMCA Camp Hayward in Cape Cod, Massachusetts, before earning a Master's degree in Art Therapy. She went on to work as a school counsellor and as Art Therapist for the Canadian Mental Health Association in Ottawa.^1

## ALS Advocacy

Skinner was diagnosed with ALS in 2013 and became a public advocate for ALS Canada, speaking about living with the disease.^1 On receiving the Pip Award, she said of the camp motto: "Never has *Non Nobis Solum* (Not For Ourselves Alone) meant so much to me."^2
