# David Leduc

*Status: draft | Sources: 3*
*Last Updated: 2026-07-11*

## Overview

David Leduc served as on-site Director of Camp Kanawana for a single summer, 2004, succeeding Morgan Carter under Executive Director Gary White. He went on to a long career in international development and philanthropic administration.

## Directorship (2004)

Leduc managed all of Kanawana's summer and winter programs — recruitment, training, and supervision of 75 employees, all operational budgets, and the safety and security of 800 participants — and developed an intensive youth leadership program, reporting to the camp's Board of Directors.^1 A 2002 dining-hall plaque names him as one of two Directors of that year's LIT program, alongside Avigail Aronoff, placing him at the camp in a staff capacity at least two years before his 2004 directorship.^2

## Later Career

Leduc holds an undergraduate degree in International Development from Dalhousie University and an MBA in Management from McGill University (2006–2011).^1 After Kanawana, he served as Oxfam-Québec's Lebanon Coordinator for the "Across Borders Project" (March 2000 – June 2002), managing programs in Palestinian refugee camps and securing $340,000 in funding.^1 He was Director of Operations at McGill University's International Community Action Network (ICAN) for approximately 11 years, and was appointed Executive Director of Development and Peace (the Canadian Catholic Organization for Development and Peace) on July 28, 2015, replacing Michael Casey, heading a team of 80 employees.^1 ^3 He later held a role as Director of Philanthropy (Quebec) at Pathways to Education Canada.^1
