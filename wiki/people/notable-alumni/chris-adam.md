# Chris Adam

*Status: draft | Sources: 6*
*Last Updated: 2026-07-11*

## Overview

Chris Adam is Coordinator of the Sustainability Office at Dawson College in Montreal, with a 35-year career in education, 25 of them at Dawson. A former Camp Kanawana staff member who ran the camp's Nature Awareness program in 1980, he received the Pip Alumni Award in 2017, recognized as the award's 10th recipient.^1 ^2

## Camp Kanawana (1980)

A "Chris Adams" from Vanier is documented in the camp's 1980 *Ka-News* as having created and run a new Nature Awareness program at Kanawana that year.^3 The surname discrepancy ("Adams" vs. "Adam") is very likely a transcription variant rather than a different person: the shared Vanier/Dawson College connection and matching wildlife and nature expertise both point to the same individual later honoured with the 2017 Pip Award.^4

## Career at Dawson College and Environmental Work

Adam has spent 25 years in Dawson College's Community Recreation and Leadership Training (CRLT) department and currently coordinates the college's Sustainability Office.^5 He is also Executive Director of the Earthvalues Institute, a non-profit organized around a "Nature as Mentor" philosophy.^5 He founded the Sustainable Happiness Certificate at Dawson and led the college's push to carbon neutrality, work that won the International Green Gown Awards' Sustainability Institution of the Year.^5 ^6 Adam holds a Master's degree in Education and has three years of wildlife and fish management experience; he received the Governor General's Meritorious Service Medal for creating Dawson's "Living Campus" sustainability model.^7 ^8
