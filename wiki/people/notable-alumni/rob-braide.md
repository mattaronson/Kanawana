# Rob Braide

## Overview

**Robert "Rob" Braide** was born April 4, 1953, in Montreal, and grew up in Westmount, attending Westmount High and Dawson College before five years at Carleton University's Faculty of Music (electronic/computer composition), which he left to take CHOM's all-night show in September 1977.^2 ^3 His broadcasting career spanned CHOM (DJ, then Music Director, then Program Director, 1977 to the mid-1980s), CKGM programming (1981), General Manager of CJAD/FM96 (renamed Mix 96) under Standard Broadcasting from June 1987, and Astral Media Group VP for branding, communications, and industry relations following Standard Radio's 2008 acquisition (laid off January 2009 in a 23-position restructuring).^2 He later served as VP of content and regulatory affairs at Stingray Digital, retiring around age 58, and now runs a consulting firm, Braide Media.^2 He was inducted into the CAB Hall of Fame (November 2009) and the Canadian Music and Broadcasting Hall of Fame, and has served on numerous community boards, including chairing Calgary's Studio Bell/National Music Centre.^2 ^3 One source states he also chaired the YMCA's Kamp Kanawana Capital Campaign — a direct institutional tie to the camp beyond being a camper alumnus, though no specific year(s) are given for this role.^2

Braide was a Camp Kanawana camper for nine summers. His testimonial, describing the formative influence of the camp, was published as recruitment material by Camp YMCA Kanawana in 2007.^1

## The 1967 Canoe Trip

In 1967, at age 14, Braide and six other boys, accompanied by a counsellor, undertook a week-long, 150-mile canoe trip from Camp Kanawana in Saint-Sauveur to Ottawa, re-tracing the historic east-west fur-trade route between Deep River and Ottawa. The group faced rapids, high waves, and long portages, sometimes canoeing until 8:00 p.m. Braide described the trip's completion as "one of the most challenging weeks of my life," with the joy and pride of arrival in Ottawa remaining "indelibly marked" in his memory.^1
