# Steve Leckman

*Status: stub | Sources: 5*
*Last Updated: 2026-07-13*

## Overview

Steve Leckman worked at Camp YMCA Kanawana starting in the summer of 2011 and went on to found a Montreal nature-connection education program, currently operating as "Programmes Coyote" / "Coyote Programs."^1 His organization is unrelated to Camp Kanawana's own, separate internal 2017 "Coyote Camp" session — the two share a similar name by coincidence, not affiliation.

## Camp Kanawana (2011)

Camp YMCA Kanawana's own Facebook page announced on May 27, 2011 that Leckman — shown that spring in the TOU.TV documentary film *Chercher le courant* — "will be working at camp next summer."^1 No further Kanawana-specific detail (role, duration, or whether he returned in later seasons) has been confirmed.

## The Coyote Club / Programmes Coyote

Leckman founded "The Coyote Club," described in a 2020 profile as a monthly nature-connection program operated through the YMCAs of Quebec, meeting in the forests of a Mohawk reserve, for children ages 7–13; he has led Montreal-area nature-connection and mentoring programs since 2011, and as of around 2020 was coordinating his first-ever week-long sleep-away camp with 40 children enrolled.^2 His organization now operates as "Programmes Coyote"/"Coyote Programs," running week-long day camps (not sleep-away) at Parc Angrignon, Montreal, for children ages 6–12, with weekly visits from Kanien'kehaka (Mohawk) knowledge keepers.^3 A 2020 social media post separately describes him as "director of Coyote Programs in Montreal," offering foraging and nature-connection workshops.^4

No source found anywhere uses the exact name "Coyote Camp" for this organization — it is consistently named "The Coyote Club" or "Programmes Coyote"/"Coyote Programs" across every source checked. This is a different, unrelated entity from Camp Kanawana's own internal 2017 "Coyote Camp" session (see [[people/notable-alumni/notable-alumni|Notable Alumni]] / the camp's own program history); no source connects Leckman to that 2017 session.^5
