# Robb Evis

*Status: stub | Sources: 6*
*Last Updated: 2026-07-13*

## Overview

Robb Evis (also spelled "Rob" informally) was a Camp Kanawana staff member in at least 1993 and 1994, per the operator's direct personal account of working alongside him at camp those years.^1 He went on to co-found Paddlefoot, an Ontario canoe-tripping and wilderness-education organization, in 1996.^2

## Camp Kanawana (1993–1994)

The operator, himself a Kanawana CIT/Junior Counsellor in 1993 and 1994 (see [[people/notable-alumni/notable-alumni|Notable Alumni]]), states with certainty that he worked alongside Evis at camp those years.^1 No independent documentary source has been found confirming this tenure — an initial 31-query, 10-surface web sweep in Campaign 74 came back null, and Evis does not appear among the 27 legible names on the 1993 "C.I.T.'s of '93" dining-hall plaque (which lists the operator himself among that year's CITs).^3 This is treated in this wiki the same way as other oral-history-only director and staff tenures (e.g. Arleen Boyer, Joanna Hoad) — recorded as a real, sourced fact at `confidence: user_knowledge`, not held to the same bar as web-documented claims.

The operator's independently recalled spelling of the name — "Robb," double-b — matches the same nickname used in Paddlefoot's own bio, a small additional point of consistency alongside the biographical plausibility of a Montreal-raised camp counsellor in his early-to-mid 20s founding an outdoor-education company two to three years later.^1 ^4

## Paddlefoot and Wilderness Safety Systems

Robb Evis is a founding member and Director/Head Instructor of Paddlefoot Inc., an Ontario wilderness adventure and canoe-tripping organization headquartered in Baysville, ON, founded in 1996 by "Robb and Sue" and federally incorporated in 1997.^2 ^4 ^5 He grew up in Montreal, Quebec, holds a Bachelor's degree from the University of Western Ontario, is a certified Ontario paramedic (part-time, Muskoka Ambulance Service), and holds instructor certifications with Rescue 3 International, Wilderness Medical Associates International, the Canadian Heart and Stroke Foundation, and Raven Rescue.^4 He also directs Wilderness Safety Systems, a Paddlefoot affiliate.^4
