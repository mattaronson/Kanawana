# John Cleghorn

*Status: draft | Sources: 6*
*Last Updated: 2026-08-28*

## Overview

John Edward Cleghorn (born July 7, 1941, Montreal) served as Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of the Royal Bank of Canada from 1994 to 2001. A Kanawana camper and a lifelong participant in canoe trips there with his own children, he received the camp's Pip Alumni Award in 2012.^1 ^2

## Early Life and Education

Cleghorn attended Westmount High School and graduated from McGill University in 1962, where he played defensive lineman on the Redmen's national championship football team; he was drafted by the Toronto Argonauts but declined to play professionally.^3 As a boy he attended Camp Kanawana, and as an adult he took his own children on canoe trips there every summer.^4 ^5 One of his summers is now fixed: a **"Rangers 56"** dining-hall plaque names **John Cleghorn** among that section's twelve boys, alongside John Bradley, Bob Brown, Frank Carpenter, Dave Chown, Dick Clements, Fred Cowie, Fred Lerner, Bill McNeil, Pete Newman, Al Whittall and Ben Hannan.^6 He would have been about fifteen, six years before graduating McGill. Whether he attended in other years is still unrecorded — no other surviving plaque names him.

## Business and Institutional Career

Cleghorn served as Chairman and CEO of the Royal Bank of Canada from 1994 to 2001, and subsequently as Chairman of Canadian Pacific Railway and of SNC-Lavalin.^3 He is an Officer of the Order of Canada and a Companion of the Canadian Business Hall of Fame, and served as Chancellor of Wilfrid Laurier University from 1996 to 2003.^3 ^1 Cleghorn chaired the Montreal YMCA and was named an Officer of YMCA Canada's Fellowship of Honour, confirming institutional ties to the organization well beyond his own camp years.^1
