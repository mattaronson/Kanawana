## Overview

Terry Mosher (born November 11, 1942, Ottawa), known professionally as Aislin, is the editorial cartoonist of the Montreal Gazette, a position he has held since 1972.^1 He is a Kanawana alumnus who attended camp as a camper in 1952–1953.^1 ^2 In 2015, he received the Pip Alumni Award and returned to Camp Kanawana for the first time in more than 60 years.^3

## Kanawana Connection

Mosher was a camper at Kanawana during the 1952 and 1953 seasons.^1 His return to camp in 2015 for the Pip Award ceremony marked a 60-year gap since his last visit.^3

## Career and Honours

Mosher has been the Montreal Gazette's editorial cartoonist since 1972, making him one of Canada's most recognized political cartoonists.^1 His honours include:

- Officer of the Order of Canada (2003)^1
- McGill University honorary doctorate (2007)^1
- Concordia University honorary doctorate (2018)^1
