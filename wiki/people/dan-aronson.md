# Dan Aronson

*Status: stub | Sources: 2*
*Last Updated: 2026-08-28*

## Overview

Dan Aronson appears in Kanawana's camp-plaque record twice, as a CIT in 1995 and as staff in 2002. No other biographical or tenure detail is currently documented.

## Documented Appearances

A "CITS 95" dining-hall plaque credits directors Simon Heller, Paula Cussen, and Wendy, and names Dan Aronson among that year's CIT cohort, alongside Sarah A. Frankel, Avigail Aronoff, Rick Badger, Laura Davis, Marc Dunn, Lindsay McDougall, Erin McKenzie, Jon Mee, Marina Sharpe, and Tom Watt.^1 Operator recall independently reaches the same cohort. Asked in August 2026 to place a former staff member at camp, Matt Aronson named the 1995 CITs as Chris Manitt, Dan Aronson, Marina Sharpe, Avigail Aronoff, Erin MacIntyre and Tiffany Ballhorn — adding that there were more he could not recall.^2 Three of those six are on the plaque; **Chris Manitt** and **Tiffany Ballhorn** are not, and since the plaque is not established as a complete roster their absence from it is not evidence against them. The sixth is unsettled: the plaque's **Erin McKenzie** and the recalled **Erin MacIntyre** share a first name and a cohort but not a surname. They are recorded here as two names, not merged into one, pending the operator's word on whether there was one Erin or two — see conflict c_027.^2

A "Staff 02" plaque lists roughly 75 names across four columns for that year, including Avigail Aronoff, Dan Aronson, Matt Aronson, David Leduc, Louis Lessard, and Helena Longpré.^1
