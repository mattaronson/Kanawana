# Dan Aronson

*Status: stub | Sources: 1*
*Last Updated: 2026-07-11*

## Overview

Dan Aronson appears in Kanawana's camp-plaque record twice, as a CIT in 1995 and as staff in 2002. No other biographical or tenure detail is currently documented.

## Documented Appearances

A "CITS 95" dining-hall plaque credits directors Simon Heller, Paula Cussen, and Wendy, and names Dan Aronson among that year's CIT cohort, alongside Sarah A. Frankel, Avigail Aronoff, Rick Badger, Laura Davis, Marc Dunn, Lindsay McDougall, Erin McKenzie, Jon Mee, Marina Sharpe, and Tom Watt.^1 A "Staff 02" plaque lists roughly 75 names across four columns for that year, including Avigail Aronoff, Dan Aronson, Matt Aronson, David Leduc, Louis Lessard, and Helena Longpré.^1
