# Dan Aronson

*Status: draft | Sources: 3*
*Last Updated: 2026-08-28*

## Overview

Dan Aronson appears in Kanawana's camp-plaque record twice, as a CIT in 1995 and as staff in 2002. No other biographical or tenure detail is currently documented. The 1995 plaque he is named on has a second significance for this project, described below: re-reading it in August 2026 corrected a transcription that had been cited as a roster for years.

## Documented Appearances

The "CITS 95" dining-hall plaque credits "Directors: Simon Heller, Paula Cussen" and names 29 CITs in four near-alphabetical blocks: Sarah A[ddleman]-Frankel, Avigail Aronoff, **Dan Aronson**, Alex Aylett, Rick Badger, Tiff Bollhorn; Nina Checcini, Ash Clark, Laura Davis, M.C. Dionne, Marc Dunn, Adriana Greenblatt, Pat Honnor, Em Lecker, Chris Manitt; Lindsay McDougall, Erin McKenzie, Jon Mee, Nick Muszynski, Melissa Pallet, Andre Preece; Marina Sharpe, Heather Sinclair, Charlie Shulman, J.M. (Jeff) Sotiron, Ben Levine, Em Watt, Tom Watt, Fabien W-Barr — followed by "& Wendy," which sits between the name column and the Directors block and may belong to either.^1 A "Staff 02" plaque lists roughly 75 names across four columns for that year, including Avigail Aronoff, Dan Aronson, Matt Aronson, David Leduc, Louis Lessard, and Helena Longpré.^1

### How that roster came to be read properly

Until August 2026 this wiki recorded only eleven of those twenty-nine names, and cited the shortened list as though it were the cohort. The full reading was prompted by memory, not by any archival discovery. Asked to place a former staff member at camp, Matt Aronson named the 1995 CITs from recall as Chris Manitt, Dan Aronson, Marina Sharpe, Avigail Aronoff, Erin MacIntyre and Tiffany Ballhorn.^2 Three matched the transcription; three did not. Two of the three misses — Manitt and Ballhorn — turned out to be on the plaque all along, in a portion of the image nobody had transcribed. The third was a genuine slip, and the operator withdrew it himself when shown the plaque: the CIT was **Erin McKenzie**, not MacIntyre (conflict c_027, resolved).^3

So the recollection was right five times out of six, and its two apparent errors were errors in this project's own reading of the source. The plaque spells the surname **Bollhorn**, and for 1995 the plaque governs.

The same pattern repeated immediately, on the neighbouring plaque and against this project's own reasoning. Re-reading "C.I.T.'s of '93" recovered three of the four worn names at the foot of its first column but left a fourth unread — and this wiki briefly argued that the plaque's alphabetical ordering *excluded* reading it as a Bollhorn, since the slot falls between Bergman and Bradbury. The operator, who is named on that plaque himself, supplied it: **Alex Bollhorn**, brother of the Tiff Bollhorn who would be a CIT two years later and staff in 1997.^3 The argument was wrong, and wrong in an instructive way. These plaques are alphabetical as a habit, not a rule; late additions get appended at the foot of a column, which is precisely where this name sits — and the 1995 plaque examined minutes earlier shows the same thing, with Ben Levine painted after Sotiron. Ordering can suggest a reading. It cannot rule one out.

The spelling is the more interesting half, because this wiki got it wrong twice before settling. The family name is **Bollhorn**, as the operator gives it and as both the 1995 and 1997 plaques clearly paint it. But the 1993 plaque appears to read **Ballhorn** — an unremarkable misspelling on a board hand-lettered by teenagers. This article first recorded that divergence, then withdrew it on the reasoning that the faint vowel had only been read *after* the operator supplied the name, making it a primed reading rather than an observation. That withdrawal was false, and checkable: the reading was reported on 28 August, two days *before* the operator named Alex at all. The operator himself pointed this out — the wrong spelling had come from his own recall of his sister's name, not from any misreading of the plaque.^3

The divergence therefore stands, recorded as found on both sides: Bollhorn in the family and on two plaques, Ballhorn in the paint of the third. What does not stand is the tidier story told about it in between — an over-correction that discarded a real observation by inventing a defect in how it had been made.^3

The two siblings bracket the mid-1990s CIT program between them: Alex in the cohort of '93, Tiff in the cohort of '95 and on staff by '97.

Both Manitt and Bollhorn appear again two years later on the "Kanawana Staff 97" plaque, alongside at least ten more of the 1995 cohort — Sarah Addleman Frankel (whose surname on the 1995 plaque is compressed to "A-Frankel"), Avigail Aronoff, Laura Davis, Marc Dunn, Adriana Greenblatt, Emily Lecker, Jonathan Mee, Nicolas Muszynski, Marina Sharpe and Charles Shulman.^1 Roughly two in five of one CIT year were still on staff two summers later — the CIT-to-staff pipeline this wiki had documented only as individual careers, visible for once as a whole cohort.
