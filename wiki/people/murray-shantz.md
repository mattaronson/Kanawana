# Murray B. Shantz

*Status: stub | Sources: 1*
*Last Updated: 2026-08-14*

## Overview

**Murray B. Shantz** was Director of Kamp Kanawana in the mid-1950s, bridging [[people/roy-locke|Roy D. Locke]] and [[people/a-ross-seaman|A. Ross Seaman]]. He is the thinnest-documented director in this index — one document names him in the role — and the article is kept short accordingly.

## Directorship (1955–1958)

The single naming source is the printed agenda of the Montreal YMCA's annual meeting of **16 April 1956**, which lists him among those leading the proceedings as:

> "MURRAY B. SHANTZ, Assistant Metropolitan Program Secretary **and Director of Kamp Kanawana**."^1

His arrival and departure are documented separately and bracket the tenure. He came from the **General Secretaryship of the Cornwall YMCA**, appointed Assistant Metropolitan Program Secretary on **15 September 1954** — filling the portfolio Roy Locke had vacated a fortnight earlier.^1 He left in 1959: "A. R. Seaman became Metropolitan Program Secretary, Secretary and Director of Boys' Camping, succeeding **Murray G. Shantz**, who joined the YMCA National Council staff."^1

That "Murray G." is an OCR or typographic slip for Murray B.; the 1962 report confirms an **M. B. Shantz** on the National Council staff.^1

**1956 is the only year stated.** The 1955, 1957 and 1958 seasons are inferred from continuity — specifically from the *absence* of any Kanawana appointment or transfer in the employed-officer change lists for 1956 and 1957. The 1958 report carries no such list at all. That is weaker evidence than it may look: it establishes that no change was *recorded*, not that no change occurred.

## Open Questions

1. [Important] Did Shantz direct the 1955, 1957 and 1958 seasons? Only 1956 is stated. No Kanawana season report survives in the digitized set for any year between 1948 and 1963, so confirmation would have to come from Concordia's physical holdings or from the *Green Triangle*, whose digitized run has nothing between 1940 and 1976.
2. [Nice-to-have] What is known of his life before Cornwall and after joining the YMCA National Council in 1959? He is the only Kanawana director in this index whose career is documented almost entirely outside Montreal.
3. [Nice-to-have] Confirm the middle initial. Every Montreal reference gives **B** except the 1959 report's "Murray G. Shantz," which is almost certainly an error — but the correction rests on the 1962 "M. B. Shantz" rather than on a source that spells the name out.
