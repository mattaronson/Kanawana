# Geoff Anderson

*Status: stub | Sources: 3*
*Last Updated: 2026-08-14*

## Overview

**Geoff Anderson** directed Kamp Kanawana for the 1969 season — a single year, begun in February and over by November. He is the director whose recovery displaced [[people/ross-bannerman|Ross Bannerman]] from this wiki's Camp Directors table, where Bannerman had sat on an inference since the project began (conflict c_023, resolved).^1

## Directorship (1969)

Anderson dates his own appointment, in the first person, in the season report he signed:

> "**I was appointed Director on February 1st, 1969.** This was to be a half-time position until May 1st and then full-time, However, the amount of work clearly indicated that the job required someone full-time so I switched fully to Kanawana on April 1st, 1969."^1

He signs that report "Geoff Anderson, Director," and a May 1969 Montreal YMCA news release independently calls him "**Geoff Anderson, Director of Kanawana**."^1 ^3

The report itself is unusual in tone. Anderson opens by warning the reader what kind of document it is: "Any report which has as its major pre-occupation giving recommendations has, of necessity, to focus on items needing improvements. Thus a report may appear to be negative. This is not my objective, many positive things happened at camp and should not be forgotten."^1 It is also the report that records **a camper drowning that summer** — the only known camper fatality in the camp's documented history. See [[history/postwar-gap|The Postwar Gap]].

## Before and After

He came to the camp from within the association: the 1967 annual report appoints him **Assistant Secretary, West Island Branch**, and the 1968 report records the transfer "G. Anderson — Associate Executive, West Island Branch, to Director, Kamp Kanawana."^2 That transfer is listed in the FY1968 report but describes a change taking effect in February 1969, which is why the 1968 season itself has no documented on-site director.

He left within the year. A French-language news release of **28 November 1969** describes him as "**Geoff Anderson, ancien directeur de Kanawana**," taking a well-earned holiday — skiing in western Canada before settling in **Toronto**.^3

**A name variant to watch for:** the 1993 alumni newsletter, listing past directors the association had lost touch with, renders him "**Jeff** Anderson."^3

## Open Questions

1. [Important] Why was the directorship a single season? Anderson describes the job outgrowing its half-time budget within two months, and the summer included a camper's death. Neither is stated as a reason for his departure, and the documents do not connect them.
2. [Important] What became of him in Toronto? The November 1969 release gives a destination and nothing else. He is the most traceable of the newly-recovered directors — a named YMCA professional with a documented move to a named city in a known year.
3. [Nice-to-have] Is "Geoff" or "Jeff" correct? Every contemporaneous Montreal document uses **Geoff**; only the 1993 alumni recollection uses Jeff. The contemporaneous spelling is followed here.
4. [Nice-to-have] Who was the camper who drowned in the summer of 1969, and what happened? Recorded in Anderson's own report without a name, date or circumstance. Handled as its own research priority, with care.
