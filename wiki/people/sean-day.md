## Overview

Sean Day served as Regional Director of Camps for the Quebec YMCAs and Director of Camp YMCA Kanawana from 2004 to approximately April 2023, a tenure of 19 years.^1 He grew up at Camp Kanawana and held a dual institutional role overseeing both the individual camp and the broader YMCA Quebec camping system.^2 His directorship spanned a period that included the Stuart McLean partnership, the COVID-19 pandemic closures, and significant post-pandemic staffing challenges.

## Directorship

Day held the dual title of "Regional Director of Camps for the Quebec YMCAs" and "Director of Camp YMCA Kanawana" simultaneously, as confirmed by multiple CBC News articles from 2017 to 2022.^2 He was present when Stuart McLean returned to camp in 2005, an event that led to the Stuart McLean Camp Kanawana Fund.^3

Day described the camp's mission: "Helping kids to understand their potential in the context of community and nature is what the camp is all about."^2

## COVID-19 Pandemic Response

During the COVID-19 pandemic, Day made the decision to close Kanawana's overnight program for two consecutive summers (2020 and 2021), citing the impossibility of offering overnight services to 250+ campers.^4 In 2021, YMCA Quebec pivoted to 10 day camps (up from 3 previously), expanding locations across Montreal, the Laurentians, and the Eastern Townships.^4

## Post-Pandemic Staffing Crisis

In May 2022, Day reported that Camp Kanawana had only hired 75% of its counsellors by the end of April, compared to the normal practice of being fully staffed by that date.^5 The YMCA made "huge increases" to salaries to recruit staff, while waiting lists for campers exceeded 100.^5

## Departure

Day left YMCA Quebec around April 2023 and subsequently became Director of Fund Development at the Rossy Foundation.^1 Kate Taylor and Kevin Slezak (Assistant Director) assumed camp leadership.^6
