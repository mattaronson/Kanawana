# Notable Alumni of Camp Kanawana

*Status: stub | Sources: 8*
*Last Updated: 2026-03-17*

## Overview

Camp Kanawana has produced a remarkable number of alumni who achieved distinction in public life. Since 2007, the Pip Alumni Award has formally recognized some of these individuals, but the camp's alumni network extends well beyond award recipients. This article collects biographical notes on notable alumni not covered by standalone articles elsewhere in the wiki.

## Pip Award Recipients Without Standalone Articles

### Richard "Itche" Kerr (Pip Award 2007)

Richard Kerr, known by his camp nickname "Itche," was the inaugural Pip Award recipient, recognized for his volunteer work with the physically challenged. He is also the composer and performer of "On My Way to Kanawana," a camp song preserved on CD in the Concordia Archives (P145/SR0001, duration 4'30"). The song is featured in the 1993 documentary "Kamp Kanawana: The Experience that Lasts a Lifetime."

### Richard Patten (1942–2021) (Pip Award 2008)

Richard Andrew Patten was a Liberal member of the Ontario Legislature representing Ottawa Centre (1987–1990, 1995–2007). He served as Minister of Government Services and Minister of Correctional Services in the Peterson government. Educated at Sir George Williams University (now Concordia), Patten worked in YMCA summer day camps in Montreal and later held positions in different YMCA branches. After leaving politics, he served as CEO of the CHEO Foundation. The Richard Patten Aboriginal Bursary Fund was created at Algonquin College in his honour. He died December 30, 2021, at age 79.

### Bruce Netherwood (Pip Award 2011)

Bruce Netherwood has spent more than 30 years in YMCA camping. He currently serves as Vice President of Camping & Social Responsibility at the South Shore YMCA in Hanover, Massachusetts, overseeing Camp Burgess & Hayward and day camps at the Hale, Emilson, and Science Center branches. He was previously Executive Director at Camp Burgess & Hayward. At Kanawana, he was staff in the late 1980s–1990s. The Concordia Archives hold an envelope addressed to him containing T-shirt design materials, confirming his operational involvement.

### John Cleghorn (Pip Award 2012)

John Edward Chicken Cleghorn (born 1941, Montreal) served as Chairman and CEO of the Royal Bank of Canada. An Officer of the Order of Canada. He attended Westmount High School and graduated from McGill University in 1962. His period as a camper likely dates to the 1950s.

### Sam Lazarus (1978–2004) (Pip Award 2013, posthumous)

Samuel Karl Lazarus was born March 3, 1978 in Montreal. He volunteered at an orphanage in Kumasi, Ghana, where he died of cerebral malaria on January 14, 2004, at age 25. His obituary in the Montreal Gazette (January 24, 2004) directed donations to the Samuel Lazarus Fund at Kamp Kanawana. The annual "Sam Jam" street hockey fundraiser has raised approximately $230,000 and sent over 50 children to camp.

### Jeniene Phillips Birks (Pip Award 2014)

Former broadcaster and community volunteer. Specific biographical details beyond her Kanawana connection and Pip Award recognition have not been documented.

### Chris Adam (Pip Award 2017)

Director of the Sustainability Office at Dawson College. Recognized as an environmental educator and former Kanawana staff member.

### Dr. Marina Sharpe (Pip Award 2018)

DPhil in Law from Oxford University (Trudeau Scholar). Associate Professor of International Law at RMC Saint-Jean. Director of the Trudeau Foundation. Author of *The Regional Law of Refugee Protection in Africa* (OUP, 2018). Former camper and Kanawana staff member.

### Dr. James Orbinski (Pip Award 2024)

Canadian physician and humanitarian who accepted the 1999 Nobel Peace Prize on behalf of Médecins Sans Frontières. Co-founded the Canadian chapter of MSF. Officer of the Order of Canada. Currently Principal of Massey College at the University of Toronto. His connection to Kanawana as camper/counsellor dates to the 1980s–1990s.

## Other Notable Alumni

### Philip "Pip" Caddell (1913–2004)

Camper in 1928. Second World War officer, Montreal executive, and community volunteer. Born July 7, 1913 in Brantford, Ontario; died July 24, 2004 in Ottawa. The Pip Award is named in his memory. See [[programs/pip-alumni-award|The Pip Alumni Award]].

### James Caddell (1973–2005)

Grandson of Pip. Camper and counsellor 1983–1991. McGill BA 1997, RMC War Studies MA. UN Peacekeeper (Bosnia). Died October 16, 2005 in Bolivia of altitude sickness. The Pip Award co-honours his memory. See [[programs/pip-alumni-award|The Pip Alumni Award]].

### R. L. Charlton (fl. 1917–1943)

Montreal marine surveyor and "ardent YMCA worker." Elected Treasurer of the Canadian Board of Marine Underwriters at its founding (March 12, 1917). Served with the YMCA in France during WWI supporting Canadian troops. Authored "Notes re Early Days of YMCA Camps at Lake St. Joseph and Kanawana" (1943), held in Concordia Archives P0145/12A.
