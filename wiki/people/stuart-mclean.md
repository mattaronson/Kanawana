# Stuart McLean

*Status: stub | Sources: 5*

## Overview

Stuart McLean (April 19, 1948 – February 15, 2017) was a Canadian author, journalist, and CBC radio host best known as the creator and host of *The Vinyl Cafe*. He worked at Camp Kanawana for five summers beginning in 1969, rising from counsellor to assistant summer camp director in 1974–1975.^1 ^2 He credited Camp Kanawana with setting him on the path to his career as a broadcaster and writer.^2

## Camp Kanawana Years

McLean was first hired as a counsellor at Camp Kanawana in 1969, at approximately age 20.^1 He worked five consecutive summers (1969–1973 as counsellor, 1974–1975 as assistant summer camp director), passing up a chance to study journalism at Carleton University to return to camp.^1 ^2

In 2005, McLean returned to Camp Kanawana for a week and hosted one of his shows there. Sean Day, then Regional Director of Camps, was present for the visit.^3

## The Stuart McLean Fund

After McLean's death in February 2017, his family established the Stuart McLean Camp YMCA Kanawana Fund to help children attend camp who lack financial means.^4 The fund raises over $150,000 per year.^4 ^5

McLean was also a recipient of the Pip Alumni Award, recognizing his contributions as a Kanawana alumnus.^5
