# The Pip Alumni Award

*Status: stub | Sources: 7*
*Last Updated: 2026-02-17*

## Overview

The Pip Alumni Award was established in 2007 as a memorial to Philip "Pip" Caddell (1913–2004) and his grandson James Caddell (1973–2005).^1 The annual award recognizes former Camp Kanawana campers and staff who embody the camp's values of service, leadership, and community engagement. The award is organized by Andrew Caddell, son of Pip and father of James.

## Namesakes

**Philip "Pip" Caddell** (July 7, 1913, Brantford, ON – July 24, 2004, Ottawa, ON) attended Camp Kanawana as a camper in 1928. He served as a WWII Canadian Army officer and later became a Montreal executive and community volunteer.^1

**James Caddell** (1973–2005) was a camper and counsellor at Kanawana from 1983 to 1991. He became a UN Peacekeeper, NGO worker, and public servant. He died of altitude sickness in Bolivia during a round-the-world trip with his wife Tara Newell.^1
