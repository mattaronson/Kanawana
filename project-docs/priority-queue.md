## Notes on the ordering

- **`p_282` is rank 1 because it is ten tasks in one envelope** — the 1936 Otoreke mixed-camp
  evaluation, the three unmirrored Kanawana sub-sub-series, the object list, the ACQ
  constitution, five physical-consultation requests, and the 1962/63 audio reels. It is also
  the only item that cannot be advanced without the operator.
- **`p_247` and `p_270` are ranked high because the Camp Lighthall work proved the method.**
  Downloading a full periodical run and grepping it end to end produced an entire article from
  a surface the open web is silent on. *The Green Triangle* is the camp's own newspaper: 38
  digitized issues exist and **six** have been read in full.
- **Blocked items stay in their tier rather than being demoted.** A blocked high-yield task is
  still the most valuable thing to unblock; hiding it at the bottom would lose that.
- Tier 3 is deliberately last but not small. Several articles are ready to advance now
  (`p_254`, `p_277`, `p_284`) and do not depend on anything above them.
