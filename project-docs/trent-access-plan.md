# Trent University Archives — the pull list

*Assembled 2026-09-06 from fifteen finding aids fetched raw from `archives.trentu.ca` and searched.
See `f_5209` through `f_5212`, and `sources/cache/trent-archives/` for the aids themselves.*

## Why this document is a pull list and not an access plan

Concordia's problem is a wall — a JavaScript challenge that refuses this environment, and a named
person who could lift it (`concordia-access-plan.md`). **Trent's problem is different and simpler:
the finding aids are open, complete and machine-readable, and the records behind them are just not
digitized.** Nothing here needs permission. It needs somebody in the reading room, or a paid
retrieval, with a list.

This is that list. It is ordered by how likely the folder is to say something about Kanawana, and
each row says what is expected and how confident that expectation is.

**Contact:** `archives@trentu.ca`, (705) 748-1011 ext. 7413. University Archivist and Head of Special
Collections: **Karen Suurtamm**. Reading-room hours, appointment requirements, remote-reference
service and copying rates are **not stated** on the archives' front page — ask in the first email.

### 3. The Centenary Journey, 1967 — twelve folders, not two

**72-007, Box 6 Folders 12-19 and Box 7 Folders 1-4.** Trip logs are named in three: F12 "personnel
data sheets, **trip logs**, area histories and trip reports"; F13 "personnel lists, correspondence,
**trip logs**"; F15 "routes, schedules, **logs**". Also F17 photographs and articles on Quetico and
the Peterborough canoe, and F19 historical essays with a **rolled map of the Centenary route in the
Map Collection**. `p_337`.

### 4. Interviews — ask for the transcripts first

**84-019, Box 2, Folder 4** — "Transcripts of some interview tapes previously recorded": Omer
Stringer · Stephen Lewis · Jean Foster · Penny Miall · Tony Fry · Camp Philosophy (CCA meeting, 1975)
· Pat Scollard · Recruiting and Public Relations (CCA Directors Conference, 1975) · Budget and
Financial Leadership (CCA Directors Conference, 1975). **Transcripts are readable where tapes are
not.**

**83-002**, *Canadian Camping Association fonds: Sound Tape Recordings* — item-level aid. The one
Quebec-connected recording is **83-002/011/008**, **Marjorie Booth**, interviewed by **Eleanor Kay**,
**12 November 1991**, on her involvement with the New Brunswick and Quebec Camping Associations and
as CCA executive director. Booth is in this wiki already, as CCA executive director and as a director
of the Montreal YWCA's Camp Oolahwan.

*Access caveat in the fonds' own words:* "In some cases, interviewers have not secured copyright
releases from the interviewees although there is a growing file of 'permission to use' forms in the
first box. All questions of copyright remain the responsibility of the researcher."

## One method note, because it nearly cost the best row above

The 85-013 aid was first read through a **summarizing** fetch, which reported "Centenary/Centennial
canoe journey records: **Absent**" and "Member camp files: **Absent**". Box 2 Folder 22 is headed
"Q.C.A. Centennial Commission" and Box 2 Folder 12 is "personnel-camps, members". **A summarizer's
negative is not the document's negative** — that is handoff rule 28. Every aid behind this list was
fetched raw and searched.

## What is not claimed

**No folder here is known to contain anything about Kanawana**, except the two in section 1 that name
it. The 1965 QCA round covered twenty-seven camps and Kanawana was a member camp of exactly that size
and type; the directories would list member camps; the English Section is where an anglophone Quebec
camp's business would sit. Those are strong expectations and none of them is a document.
