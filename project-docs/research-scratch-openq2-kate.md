**Surface:** WebSearch
**Results:** RELEVANT — Camp Kanawana introduced a co-ed "gender-expansive" tent, open to all. It accommodates transgender, non-binary, and gender-questioning campers. Previously, such campers chose boys' or girls' tents. Male counsellors may work with girls (and vice-versa); all campers participate in all activities regardless. The tent choice is purely about sleeping comfort. Kate Taylor was involved in discussing/implementing this initiative.

## Consolidated Findings: Kate Taylor

### Identity
- **Full name:** Kate Taylor (middle initial W. per LinkedIn handle "katewtaylor")
- **Camp alias/nickname:** Wawa
- **Social media:** @thiskatetaylor (X/Twitter); LinkedIn: linkedin.com/in/katewtaylor
- **Location:** Montreal, Quebec area (inferred from role)

### Education
- **Honours BA in Psychology** from Trent University (Peterborough, Ontario)

### Professional Roles
1. **Camp Director, Camp YMCA Kanawana** (YMCAs of Quebec) — 2020s; exact start date not determined but she was director during both the 2020 and 2021 COVID closures
2. **Camp Director, Camp Ta-Wa-Si Inc.** — dates unknown; Camp Ta-Wa-Si is located at Johnston's Point, Northumberland Strait, New Brunswick (founded 1940, turned 60 in 2000)
3. **Consultant & Graphic Designer, Stephane Richard Development Consulting** — dates unknown; appears to be concurrent/consulting role
4. **Podcast contributor/editor** for Go Camp Pro — described as one of the editors

### Skills and Expertise
- Program Management
- Staff Engagement
- Leadership Training
- Web Design
- Adobe Photoshop, InDesign, Illustrator
- Content creation and web management
- Social media advocacy

### Podcast Appearances
1. **Go Camp Pro "Beyond Camp" #18** — "Social Media Advocacy - with Kate Taylor" — discussed exploring privilege and using it to advocate for others
2. **CampHacker #147** — "Summer Self-Care for Camp Directors - with Kate Taylor" — frank discussion about prioritizing self-care as a camp director
3. **Camp Code #95** — "How to Combat Learning Loss in Your Staff Team - with Kate Taylor" — guest consultant on staff training

### Initiatives at Kanawana
- Led introduction of **gender-expansive tent option** — co-ed sleeping arrangement for transgender, non-binary, and gender-questioning campers, or anyone who prefers it
- Part of leadership team during **COVID-19 pandemic response** (2020-2021 closures)
- Participated in **Zoom town hall meetings** with camp families (January 27, year likely 2022)
- Emphasized camp values of **inclusivity, community, and safe spaces**
