## Summary of All Known Facts About Mabel C. Jamieson

### Confirmed (from Camp Ouareau official sources + Wikipedia):
1. Co-founded Camp Ouareau (1922) with Ferna G. Halliday
2. Both described as "school teachers" and "university women and teachers"
3. Both were "residents of Montreal" at the time of founding
4. "Had considerable experience of camping and summer camp activities" before 1922
5. In 1921, they acquired land from Mr. Leguerrier on Lac Ouareau
6. Under Quebec law, needed fathers' permission to buy property
7. Co-directed Camp Ouareau 1922-1923 with Halliday
8. Sole director of Camp Ouareau from 1924 to 1934
9. Succeeded as director by Dorothy Percival in 1934
10. Was "National Student Secretary of the Christian Association of Young Women of Canada" in 1915
11. "After completing her studies in 1918 at Pupils College in Richmond" (source: Camp Ouareau official page)
12. "Became secretary general of the Y.M.C.A. of Montreal" (source: Camp Ouareau official page — unverified, possibly YWCA)
13. Member of the "Committee of the Sixteen" (Comite des seize) in 1919
14. Described as a "pioneering figure in girls' camping and women's empowerment"
15. Original intention was "to provide young women an experience of nature and outdoor activities before their marriage"

### Unverified:
1. **YMCA vs YWCA**: The claim she was "secretary general of the YMCA of Montreal" has never been independently verified. The title "secretary general" (or "general secretary") was typically the YMCA's chief executive — it would be extraordinary for a woman to hold this role in 1915-1920. Far more likely she held a senior role at the YWCA, given that:
   - Her 1915 role was at the Christian Association of Young Women (YWCA)
   - Her co-founder Ferna Halliday was YWCA Montreal secretary 1918-1921
   - Their colleague Mary Edgar was Girls' Work Secretary of YWCA Montreal 1915-1919
   - All three women (Jamieson, Halliday, Edgar) were in the same YWCA network
   - No independent YMCA record names Jamieson

2. **FamilySearch Mabel Grace Jamieson (1893-1966)**: ID L2Y3-FWD. Dates would fit (age 22 in 1915 as YWCA secretary, age 29 founding camp in 1922, age 41 succeeded by Percival in 1934). BUT: middle name "Grace" does not match known initial "C." AND multiple people named Mabel Grace Jamieson existed across North America (Michigan, Saskatchewan). FamilySearch page is 403-blocked.

3. **"Pupils College in Richmond"**: Unidentifiable institution. St. Francis College in Richmond, QC closed in 1898 — 20 years before Jamieson's alleged 1918 studies. May be a mistranslation from French, or a very small/short-lived institution. No web trace found.

4. **Birth/death dates**: Completely unknown from web sources. If the FamilySearch record is correct: 1893-1966.

5. **OCA membership**: Jamieson is NOT listed as a founding member of the Ontario Camping Association (1933). That was Halliday, along with A.L. Cochrane, H.E. Chapman, Mary Edgar, Mary Hamilton, and Taylor and Ethel Statten.

### New Leads from This Session:

1. **Baltimore Sun "MABEL C." obituary (June 30, 1985)**: Found on newspapers.com (article ID 112454903). Preview only shows "Obituary for MABEL C." — full surname and details are behind paywall. Likely a different person (Baltimore, not Montreal), but worth checking if accessible.

2. **Comite des seize founding date correction**: Academic article on erudit.org confirms the committee was founded in 1917 (not 1918 as some sources claim). This is consistent with Jamieson's 1919 membership. The committee was supported by the Montreal Local Council of Women.
