# Spinout audit: sections that have outgrown their article

Generated by `scripts/wiki/spinout_audit.py` on 2026-09-06. Regenerate rather than hand-edit.

**The principle** (operator direction, 2026-09-06): a reference point inside an article spins out
into its own article when it has its own arc rather than a role in the parent's arc, when three or
more articles would want to link to it, or when its section has outgrown a paragraph and a link.
The parent keeps enough to still read whole, and links out. Directors already work this way —
`directors-index.md` names them, and each has their own article. Nothing else does.

**What this measures.** Section word counts, which is a proxy and only a proxy. A 1,200-word
section that is genuinely one argument belongs where it is. A 400-word section about a person whose
career ran elsewhere may still want its own article and will not appear here. This flags candidates
for a human to judge; it does not decide.

**Scale of the problem.** Median article: 1,892 words. Largest: 36,649. 63 sections across 36 of
the 108 articles are over 900 words; 29 sections across 21 articles are over 1,500.
