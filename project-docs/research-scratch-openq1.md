   - 125th (2019): "Family, Alumni, and Friends Weekend" at camp (on-site, summer)
   - The 100th (1994) format is unknown but could have been either off-site reunion or on-site summer event

5. **Bruce Netherwood confirmed as Pip Award recipient** and current VP at South Shore YMCA (Hanover, MA). He would be the primary living witness to centennial planning and events. LinkedIn profile exists.
