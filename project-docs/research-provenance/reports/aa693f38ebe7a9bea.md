**Research findings: Camp Weredale and Camp Kanawana relationship**

The existing article at `/home/user/Kanawana/wiki/context/camp-weredale.md` (E1-reviewed) already captures essentially everything discoverable through online sources. My searches across 15+ distinct queries and multiple search surfaces confirmed the existing KB and surfaced no new facts about shared staff, programming traditions, or details of the two-site plan. Here is the summary:

**What is established:**
- Camp Weredale was never a YMCA property. Founded 1934 by the Rotary Club of Westmount for Weredale House (Boys' Home of Montreal, est. 1870). Located on Lac l'Achigan, Saint-Hippolyte -- a different lake and municipality from Kanawana (Lac Kanawana/Wilson, Saint-Sauveur).
- In 1977, Weredale House merged into Youth Horizons; Camp Weredale was spun off to the independent Weredale Foundation, which still operates it.
- The YMCA of Montreal produced situation reports profiling Kanawana, Otoreke, and Weredale together (c.1977-1980) and proposed a two-site operation. The plan was abandoned; no online source documents why.
- The only documented personnel crossover is Frank Edward Crabbe (1946-1966), a Weredale Boy and Camp Lewis counsellor, killed in Vietnam -- connecting the two communities but not demonstrating institutional staff sharing.

**What remains unanswerable online:**
- Why the two-site plan was abandoned (requires Concordia Archives P0145/12A, 1977-1983 documents)
- Whether staff or programming was shared (no staff names from either camp's 1970s era are indexed online)
- The operational details of what the combined camp would have looked like

**One new minor detail:** The Weredale "Old Boys" held an 85th reunion in 2019 and a 90th anniversary in 2024 with alumni event, staff BBQ, and sunset campfire -- confirming an active alumni network that could potentially be a source for oral history about any Kanawana connections.
