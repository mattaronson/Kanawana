**Camp Otoreke -- Consolidated Findings:**

All findings below are already captured in the existing article `/home/user/Kanawana/wiki/places/camp-otoreke.md` (E1-reviewed), **except one possible new detail**:

- **Already documented**: Founded 1894 as Camp Jubilee; renamed Otoreke 1909; located on islands in Lake Saint-Joseph, Saint-Adolphe-d'Howard; three islands plus mainland landing; YMCA boys' camp relocated to Kanawana 1910; thereafter served adults (men 18+, mixed adults, married couples, low-income families); 1944 photograph exists; 1967 Centennial Conference; closed 1982; sold 1987; Concordia Archives sub-series P0145/12C.
- **Potentially new detail from BaladoDiscovery search snippet**: "In 1897, the YMCA acquired two islands of Lake Saint-Joseph" (the existing article says "three islands" were purchased, citing QAHN; the 1897 date for acquisition is not in the current article -- it only says 1894 founding). - **Name meaning clarification**: BaladoDiscovery says "north" in Iroquois language; blog says "home in the north." The existing article says "Iroquoian for 'north'" which aligns with BaladoDiscovery.
