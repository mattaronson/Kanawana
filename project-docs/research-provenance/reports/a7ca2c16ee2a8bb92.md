## Research Summary: Visual & Material Culture — Camp Kanawana (p_070)

**Six targets searched, two positive hits, two dead ends, two partial/clarified, plus one major bonus finding.**

### Positive Results

1. **Flickr**: Official camp account holds 4,416 photos (primarily modern camp life, not historical).
2. **McCord Stewart Museum**: Collection **C387 — "Kamp Kanawana"**, circa 1930 photographs. No images digitized online; requires direct museum inquiry.

### Dead Ends

3. **eBay / Auctions**: Zero listings for Kanawana memorabilia on any indexed marketplace.
4. **Library and Archives Canada**: No Kanawana holdings found.

### Partial / Clarified

5. **Historical Topographic Maps**: The relevant NTS sheet is **021L13 (Saint-Raymond)**, not 21M. Historical editions (1948+) available through Borealis Data and NRCan Toporama.
6. **CCA / Ross & Macdonald**: The firm designed several urban Montreal YMCA buildings (1909-1913) but **no camp buildings** appear in the indexed fonds. The Ross & Macdonald connection to Kanawana is unconfirmed.

### Major Bonus: Concordia University Archives

The primary repository is the **YMCA of Montreal Fonds (P0145/12B)** at Concordia — 7.72 metres of Kanawana material including photographs (c.1936+), film reels, brochures (1921-2001), the "Green Triangle" newsletter (1932-1940), blueprints, posters, badges, pennants, t-shirts, and a 1993 VHS documentary. Five items are already digitized on the Internet Archive, notably a 1923 camp brochure and a 1935 camp history.

### Top Priorities for Follow-Up
