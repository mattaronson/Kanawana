### 2. **Move from Lake Saint-Joseph to Saint-Sauveur (1909-1910)**

**Finding:** Camp Jubilee began in 1894 on islands in Lake Saint-Joseph. Camp Otoreke (renamed from Jubilee in 1909) continued operating there until 1982. Camp Kanawana was established at the separate Saint-Sauveur location acquired from the Page family, but the exact timing of the move/transfer is not explicitly stated in public sources.

**Key detail:** Camp Otoreke and Camp Kanawana appear to be separate operations in different locations.

### 6. **Camp Weredale - Site & Two-Site Plan (1977-1980)**
