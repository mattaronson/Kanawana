### **FOR BILLY BALL:**

**Claim 1: The 1894 trip had 20 boys going to an island on Lake Saint-Joseph in the Township of Howard**
- ✅ **CONFIRMED** - Billy Ball article states: "taking a group of 20 boys to an island on Lake Saint-Joseph in the Township of Howard (now Saint-Adolphe-d'Howard)"
- KB fact confirmed: "Billy Ball formally established Camp Jubilee in summer 1894 with 20 campers"

**Claim 2: D.A. Budge — Daniel Andrew Budge, born 1851 in Newmarket ON, died 1933, served as General Secretary Montreal YMCA from 1874 to 1913**
- ✅ **CONFIRMED** - Billy Ball article: "D.A. Budge (Daniel Andrew Budge, 1851-1933, who served from 1874 to 1913)"
- KB fact confirmed: "D.A. Budge's full name was Daniel Andrew Budge. Born April 16, 1851 in Newmarket, Ontario. Died 1933. Served as General Secretary of the Montreal YMCA from 1874 to 1913."

**Claim 3: In 1892, Lemuel Cushing brought boys to Lake Saint-Joseph (2 years before Ball's trip)**
- ✅ **CONFIRMED** - Billy Ball article: "In 1892, two years before Ball's trip, Lemuel Cushing brought a group of boys to Lake Saint-Joseph"
- KB fact confirmed: "Lemuel Cushing brought the first group to Lake St. Joseph in 1892"

**Claim 4: Camp Jubilee was renamed Camp Otoreke in 1909, closed 1982**
- ✅ **CONFIRMED** - Billy Ball article: "In 1909, Camp Jubilee was renamed **Camp Otoreke**" and "Camp Otoreke closed in 1982"
- Camp Otoreke article states the same
- KB fact confirmed: "In 1909, Camp Jubilee was renamed Camp Otoreke (Iroquoian for 'north')...Camp Otoreke closed in 1982."

**Claim 5: The Montreal YMCA was founded November 25, 1851, first in North America**
- ✅ **CONFIRMED** - Billy Ball article: "The Montreal YMCA, founded November 25, 1851, was the first YMCA in North America."

**Claim 6: The YMCA was founded in 1844 in London (making 1894 the jubilee/50th anniversary)**
- ✅ **CONFIRMED** - Billy Ball article: "The camp was named to commemorate the 50th anniversary of the worldwide YMCA movement (founded 1844 in London)"
- KB fact: "Camp Jubilee was named for the YMCA 50th anniversary (YMCA founded 1844, jubilee year 1894)"
