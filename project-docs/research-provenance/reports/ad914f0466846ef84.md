## Research Report: Edgar E. Smee

### Confirmed Facts (from web searches and existing KB)

**Full name:** Edgar E. Smee (middle name unknown -- only the initial "E." is documented). Known informally as "Ed Smee."

**Birth:** Circa 1912-1913, Hamilton, Ontario (calculated from age 89 at death in February 2002).

**Death:** February 7, 2002, at the Glebe Centre in Ottawa, Ontario, at age 89. Obituary published in *The Globe and Mail*, February 11, 2002.

**Family:**
- Wife: Frieda
- Daughters: Linda, Susan, Sydney, and Sonia
- Brothers: Norman and Leonard
- Memorial gathering: February 16, 2002, at Workers Arts and Heritage Centre, 51 Stuart Street, Hamilton

**Camp Kanawana Service:**
- 1939: Juvenile section director (source: McMorris thesis, 2023)
- 1942: "Resident Director" alongside Camp Director R.H. Hanagan (source: *Montreal Gazette*, August 5, 1942, "Y.M.C.A. Boys Stage Parade and Circus")

**Environmental Career (Hamilton):**
- Grew up swimming in Red Hill Creek as a boy
- Left Hamilton c. 1938 (age ~25)
- Returned to Hamilton in 1968 after a 30-year absence with wife Frieda
- Visited Red Hill Creek and found it polluted by a storm sewer outlet; beaches also polluted -- this "sparked a deep anger" in him
- Described as having been "raised to treasure, not squander, the earth's resources"
- 1969: Co-founded **Clear Hamilton of Pollution (CHOP)**, a grassroots environmental group
- 1983: Received the **Dr. Victor Cecilioni Environmentalist of the Year Award** (Hamilton's highest environmental honour, given annually since 1979, co-sponsored by Conserver Society, Hamilton Naturalists' Club, Hamilton Conservation Authority, Environment Hamilton, and Bay Area Restoration Council)
- 1984: CHOP incorporated as **Conserver Society of Hamilton & District Inc.** -- Smee served as Founding Chair
- Helped create the first action plan for Hamilton Harbour/Burlington Bay
- Conserver Society activities under his leadership: composting programs, bicycle paths advocacy, pesticide reduction, urban sprawl limitation, waste management, air quality monitoring, transportation
- 1986: Initiated the Conserver Society's first Environmental Trust Fund
- 1999: Fund renamed **Ed Smee Conserver Society Environmental Fund** in his honour, transferred to Hamilton Community Foundation (still active)
