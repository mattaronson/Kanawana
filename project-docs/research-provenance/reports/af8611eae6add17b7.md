## Visual and Material Culture Search: Summary of Key Findings

### Strongest Discoveries

**Architecture**: The single most significant find is that the **Canadian Centre for Architecture holds Ross & Macdonald architectural drawings for a Doctor's Cottage at Kamp Kanawana**. Ross & Macdonald was the firm behind the Royal York Hotel, Union Station Toronto, and the T. Eaton stores -- one of the most prestigious architectural practices in early 20th-century Canada. That they designed a building for a summer camp is a notable connection. The firm was active 1913-1942, so the cottage dates to that window. The CCA finding aid is not fully available online; in-person consultation would be needed to get dates, drawing details, and whether other Kanawana commissions exist.

**Film and Audio**: Two primary audiovisual sources surfaced:
- A **1993 documentary** directed by Cathy Reeves, *Kamp Kanawana: The Experience that Lasts a Lifetime*, held in the Concordia archives with a YouTube access copy. It describes Kanawana as the oldest camp in Quebec and second oldest in Canada.
- A **1941 CFCF radio broadcast** about Kamp Kanawana, held on the Internet Archive via Concordia University Records Management. CFCF was Montreal's pioneering radio station. This is an unusually early piece of camp-related broadcast media.

**Stuart McLean**: The search filled out the biography of a major Kanawana alumnus. McLean was a counsellor for five summers starting in 1969 and assistant camp director in 1974-1975. He credited the camp with transforming his life. After his death in 2017, his family established the Stuart McLean Camp YMCA Kanawana Fund. He warrants his own article stub.

**Photography**: A Flickr account under the name "Kanawana" hosts **4,416 photos**. The Instagram account (@ymcakanawana) has 571 posts and markets itself around "125+ years of memories." Neither has been reviewed for historical content versus contemporary promotional material.

### Moderate Finds

**Maps**: Camp Kanawana holds official place-name status in the Canadian Geographical Names Database (ID: EKWXQ). A bathymetric chart of Lac Kanawana is available. No historical property maps or period topographic sheets of the camp area surfaced online.

**Social Media**: The camp maintains accounts on Facebook (3,549 likes, 2,823 check-ins), Instagram, TikTok, and X/Twitter. No dedicated alumni Facebook group was found via search (one may exist as a private group). No Reddit threads about Camp Kanawana exist. The Facebook page confirmed details about the Pip Award (annual, alumni committee, given to former camper/counsellor/supervisor exemplifying selflessness) and the 120th anniversary reunion at Victoria Hall on September 5, 2014.

**Traditions and Material Culture**: The camp tuck shop currently sells swag; every camper receives a water bottle on arrival and a t-shirt and group photos on departure. The camp motto "Non Nobis Solum" (Not for ourselves alone) was confirmed.

### Null Results

**Merchandise and Memorabilia**: This was the weakest category. No Camp Kanawana items appeared on eBay, Etsy, or WorthPoint. No vintage pennants, badges, or patches surfaced anywhere. A c1915 Valcartier YMCA Camp postcard on eBay shows the tradition existed for Quebec YMCA camps, but nothing Kanawana-specific has entered the online collectibles market. This is either because such items are rare, or because they circulate privately among alumni rather than through dealers.

**Institutional Collections**: McCord Museum and Library and Archives Canada returned nothing. The McCord holds 2.15 million photographs and could contain relevant material, but nothing is indexed under Kanawana in web-searchable form.

**YouTube**: No dedicated Camp Kanawana YouTube channel or historical footage surfaced beyond the Concordia-archived documentary.
