### Search Limitations

1. **Newspapers.com paywall**: Only 5 Gazette clippings surfaced via web search. A paid Newspapers.com subscription would likely reveal many more articles across the 1890s-2000s. The Concordia archives finding aid references "articles and ads clippings from 1968-1973" and "newspaper advertising from 1936" -- these physical clippings may correspond to articles discoverable on Newspapers.com.

2. **BAnQ Numerique**: The digital portal of Quebec's national library holds OCR-searchable runs of La Presse, Montreal Herald, and The Gazette. A direct search on https://numerique.banq.qc.ca/ for "Kanawana" could yield French-language newspaper mentions not findable via Google. This is a high-priority research avenue.

3. **Google News Archive**: The discontinued Google News Archive did not return Kanawana-specific results through web search. Direct access at https://news.google.com/newspapers may yield additional historical articles.

4. **Montreal Star**: No Montreal Star articles were found. The Star ceased publication in 1979. Its archives may be accessible through BAnQ or microfilm collections. Given Kanawana's prominence in anglophone Montreal, Star coverage almost certainly exists for the 1920s-1970s period.
