### Key Clarification: Camp Weredale vs. YMCA
Camp Weredale is **not** a YMCA camp. While archival records at Concordia show historical connections (both camps appear in YMCA of Montreal fonds documents from mid-20th century), Camp Weredale today operates independently under the Weredale Foundation. The only YMCA camp currently operating in Quebec is **Camp Kanawana**.
