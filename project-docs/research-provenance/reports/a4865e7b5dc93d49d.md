## Key Findings

### NEW Finding (not in existing KB or article)

**Harold C. Cross was based in Victoria, British Columbia from 1919 to 1925 before coming to Montreal.** This is documented by an archival item: "Personal Reminiscences-Victoria, B.C. 1919-25, by Harold C. Cross" (written in 1967), held in the Concordia University Archives, YMCA of Montreal fonds P0145, sub-series 14H. This document is filed among "Local YMCA Histories" and strongly suggests Cross was a YMCA staff member (likely Boys' Work Secretary or similar) at the Victoria, BC YMCA before transferring to Montreal around 1925, which aligns perfectly with his documented role as Camp Kanawana director starting approximately 1925.

This establishes a career trajectory: Victoria BC YMCA (1919-1925) --> Montreal YMCA / Camp Kanawana director (c. 1925-1928) --> National Council Boys' Work involvement (1938-1945) --> YMCA institutional historian (1949-1951+).

2. **World Alliance correspondence 1939-1943** - Confirmed in sub-sub-series 14A01, indicating Cross had international YMCA connections during the WWII period.
