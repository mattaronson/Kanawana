## Phase A: ANALYZE — All Facts Found

### FACT 1: Title and Documented Years of Service
- **Claim:** R.H. Hanagan served as "Montreal YMCA camp director" in 1942 and 1946.
- **Sources:** src_mcmorris_thesis (f_0043, f_0698), src_concordia_fonds (f_0356, f_0404, f_0929)
- **Confidence:** STATED
- **Note:** The title is "Montreal YMCA camp director" — not "Kanawana camp director" — implying system-wide oversight of YMCA camping operations.

### FACT 2: Two-Tier Directorship Structure (1942)
- **Claim:** In 1942, R.H. Hanagan held the title "Camp Director" while E.E. Smee held the title "Resident Director." This documents a two-tier leadership structure during the wartime period.
- **Sources:** src_gazette_1942_parade (f_0738, f_0851, f_0855)
- **Confidence:** STATED (newspaper primary source)
- **Note:** Smee was the on-site leader; Hanagan held the broader supervisory role.

### FACT 3: The 1942 Circus and Parade
- **Claim:** A Montreal Gazette article dated August 5, 1942, titled "Y.M.C.A. Boys Stage Parade and Circus," describes Hanagan "in Indian garb on horseback." The camp operated with Bantam, Junior, Intermediate, and Senior sections.
- **Sources:** src_gazette_1942_parade (f_0851)
- **Confidence:** STATED (primary source)

### FACT 4: 1946 Polio Outbreak Response
- **Claim:** Six polio cases occurred at Kanawana in 1946. Dr. Cushing (identified as a pediatrician) visited the camp and recommended cancelling canoe trips but keeping the camp open. Three of six cases were discharged by end of summer.
- **Sources:** src_mcmorris_thesis (f_0224, f_0684)
- **Confidence:** STATED
- **Note:** Hanagan managed this crisis as camp director (f_0044, f_0698). The Dr. Cushing here is a pediatrician, not the W.E. Cushing who wrote historical sketches in 1943 — this distinction should be noted to avoid confusion with other Cushing-family articles.

### FACT 5: Season Reports in Concordia Archives
- **Claim:** Hanagan wrote detailed season reports preserved in the Concordia University Archives (P0145/12A). His report is listed near 1946 materials.
- **Sources:** src_concordia_fonds (f_0356, f_0404)
- **Confidence:** STATED

### FACT 6: Broader YMCA Camp Oversight (Inferred)
- **Claim:** The Concordia finding aid (12A) places Hanagan's report alongside system-wide documents: "Comparison of Two Boys Camps operated by Montreal YMCA" (Kanawana and Camp Perrot, c.1945-46), "Report of the Montreal YMCA Camps for Boys and Girls" (1946), and an ACA Conference report to the Camp Board of Management (1946). This confirms Hanagan's portfolio extended beyond Kanawana alone.
- **Sources:** src_concordia_fonds, src_concordia_ymca_12A
- **Confidence:** INFERRED (from archival grouping context)

### FACT 7: Predecessor Context
- **Claim:** Howie Langille was Camp Chief/Director in 1941 (the 48th season, with 68 staff and 30+ wartime evacuees from Britain). No director is documented between Langille (1941) and Hanagan (1942), suggesting direct succession.
- **Sources:** src_cfcf_1941 (f_0047, f_0048, f_0092)
- **Confidence:** INFERRED

### FACT 8: E.E. Smee Career Context
