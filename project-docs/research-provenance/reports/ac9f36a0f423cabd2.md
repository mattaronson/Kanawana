## Summary Assessment

The online newspaper search space for Camp Kanawana is largely exhausted. The 7 known Gazette articles (1897-1974) appear to be all that Newspapers.com has digitized. The major gaps -- 1920s, 1950s, 1960s -- likely reflect either (a) no articles published, (b) articles not yet digitized, or (c) articles behind paywalls on platforms not indexed by web search. The richest untapped vein remains the **Concordia University Archives (YMCA of Montreal fonds)**, which holds physical newspaper clippings from 1968-1973 and tear sheets from 1993-1994. The **Montreal Star** remains a complete blind spot due to lack of digitization. BAnQ Numerique may have additional La Presse or other French-language coverage not surfaced by web search.
