### Stuart McLean's Time at Kanawana - Consolidated Details

- **Born**: April 19, 1948, Montreal West
- **First visited Kanawana**: Summer 1969, visiting a friend who was working there
- **Hired**: On the spot as counsellor in the senior boys' section, summer 1969
- **Duration**: Five summers total (1969, ~1970, ~1971, ~1972/73, 1974 or 1975)
- **Role progression**: Counsellor (senior boys' section) -> Assistant Summer Camp Director (summers 1974 and 1975)
- **Education**: BA in Applied Social Science from Sir George Williams University (now Concordia), 1971
- **Passed up**: An opportunity to study journalism at Carleton University to return to camp as assistant director
- **Key quotes**: "When I got to camp, I found a place where I was accepted by others"; "Camp is where I found my confidence"; "an utter academic, athletic and social failure" before camp; "I began pulling my adult self together at Camp Kanawana"
- **Post-camp**: Worked at Dawson College Student Services, edited the Dawson Forum, managed Nick Auf der Maur's city council campaign, entered CBC Radio 1974 as researcher for Cross Country Checkup
- **Lived on Dorchester**: After university, while at Dawson College; sublet the apartment to a Kanawana colleague (the anonymous director) when he moved to Toronto
- **Pip Alumni Award**: 2009
- **Stuart McLean Fund**: Established before his death (Feb 15, 2017); raises over $150,000/year for financially disadvantaged campers
- **2005 return visit**: Spent a week at Kanawana, hosted a Vinyl Cafe show there
