## A: ANALYZE -- Extracted Facts

### What was Camp Perrot?
- **A boys' camp operated by the Montreal YMCA alongside Kamp Kanawana** (f_0874, stated confidence, src_concordia_atom_fonds)
- **A separate YMCA camp from Kanawana** (f_0330, stated confidence, src_concordia_fonds)
- It had its own dedicated archival sub-series: **P0145/12G Camp Perrot** (f_0871)

### Where was it located?
- The name strongly suggests a connection to **Ile Perrot**, a large island at the confluence of the Ottawa River and the St. Lawrence, approximately 40 km west of Montreal
- Ile Perrot was also a **canoe trip destination** for Kanawana campers: "7-8 day trips to Ile Perrot and Grenville for intermediates" by 1945 (f_0676, McMorris thesis)
- No archival source explicitly states the camp's location on Ile Perrot, but the name and geographic context make this highly probable

### When did it operate?
- The earliest documentary evidence is a **"Comparison of Two Boys Camps operated by Montreal YMCA" dated approximately 1945-1946** (f_0897, f_0874)
- A second comparison document dates from **1953-1954** (f_0874)
- Camp Perrot is **absent from 1980 situation reports** which mention Kanawana, Otoreke, and Weredale, suggesting it had closed by then
- **Probable operating period: mid-1940s through sometime before 1980**, possibly only the 1940s-1950s

### Relationship to Kanawana
- Both were boys' camps under the same YMCA Montreal camping program
- The YMCA produced formal comparison documents between the two camps (1945-46 and 1953-54), suggesting they were evaluated as peer facilities
- Ile Perrot was also a canoe trip destination from Kanawana, raising the question of whether Camp Perrot served as a waypoint or base for intermediate canoe trips
- Camp Perrot appears in the same archival series (P0145/12) alongside Kanawana (12B), Otoreke (12C), Becsies (12D), Dorval (12E), Weredale (12F), and others

### Why is it in the YMCA of Montreal fonds?
- Because it was operated by the YMCA of Montreal as part of their camping program. The fonds at Concordia holds records for all YMCA Montreal camps.

### Contradictions / Flags
- **Date discrepancy in f_0346**: Claims the comparison document exists "for 1945-46 and 1950" while f_0897 and f_0874 say "1945-46" and "1953-54." The "1950" reference may be a misread or a third document (a 1950 Boys' Camping Committee Survey exists separately). This should be flagged but is non-blocking.
