**Facts extracted:**

Camp layout and sections:
- **Front Camp**: houses the Farmhouse and Blockhouse -- winterized with kitchens, bathrooms, showers, electricity
- **Woodsmen and Pioneers sections** (junior): 12 cabins with electricity, each accommodating 10 campers + 2 staff
- **Coureurs des Bois and Pathfinders sections** (senior): 14 prospector tents (wooden platform with bunk beds, heavy canvas tent and waterproof tarp), 8 campers + 1-2 staff per tent
- **Centre Camp**: small cabins (no electricity/plumbing) and platform tents (KB f_0028)
- Current section names and demographics: Pathfinders and Pioneers (girls/non-binary); Coureurs des Bois and Woodsmen (boys/non-binary); Senior Kanawanians and Voyageurs (co-ed)
- Additional senior groups: Rangers, Trailblazers, Foresters, Adventurers

Activity and trail facilities:
- **Nature interpretation trails** across 500+ acres of mature forest (no individual trail names published)
- New orienteering methods with a nature trail introduced in **1980**
- Canoe trip programs in **Papineau-Labelle Park** (Adventurer program: 22 km over 4 days, portages 100m-690m between Lac Saint-Denis, Lac du Crochet, Lac Montjoie)
- Waterfront: sand beach with marked swimming area, canoes, kayaks, paddleboards, water trampoline, barge, rescue boat

Common buildings:
- **Dining Hall/Cafeteria**: capacity 300+, industrial kitchen (not available to rental groups)
- **Katimavik**: indoor gymnasium
- **Arts and crafts pavilion**
- **Beach lodge**
- **Nature nook** (nature interpretation area)
- **Activity lodge**
- **Rose des Vents Pavilion**: kitchen for max 16, wood-burning stove, common area; available in "refuge mode" (no running water) Oct 15 - May 20
- **Infirmary**: 5 beds, private washroom, emergency oxygen tank, AED
- Gaga ball pit, low ropes course, archery range, activity/sports field
