- **I could not independently verify this claim.** The LinkedIn page itself returned 403 when fetched.

### 2020 and 2021: Almost certainly no award given

Camp Kanawana was **closed for both the 2020 and 2021 summers** due to COVID-19 (confirmed by CBC News reporting). Since the Pip Award ceremony occurs at camp during the summer season and again at the September alumni gathering, it is highly likely no award was given in either year.

### 2022 and 2023: Unknown

No information was found about recipients for these years. Camp Kanawana reopened for summer 2022 (staff returned in 2023 per the YMCA team page). The absence of YMCA Quebec press releases for 2022 and 2023 (compared to releases for 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, and 2024) is notable. Possible explanations:
- The award was on hiatus and only resumed in 2024
- The press releases exist but are not indexed by search engines
- The award was given but not announced via press release
