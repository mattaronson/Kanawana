## What We Already Know (from KB facts)

- R.H. Hanagan served as Montreal YMCA Camp Director in **1942** and **1946**
- He succeeded Howie Langille (director in 1941) in what appears to be a direct succession
- In 1942, he and Resident Director E.E. Smee organized a circus and parade at Kamp Kanawana; Hanagan appeared "in Indian garb on horseback" (Montreal Gazette, August 5, 1942)
- He wrote detailed season reports and managed the **1946 polio outbreak**
- He wrote a **1946 report to the Camp Board of Management on the American Camping Association Conference**
- The Concordia Archives (P0145, sub-series 12A) hold his report alongside a "Comparison of Two Boys Camps operated by Montreal YMCA" (Kanawana and Camp Perrot, c.1945-46) and a "Report of the Montreal YMCA Camps for Boys and Girls" (1946)
- He produced a report for the **Boys' Camping Committee-Survey Committee in 1950**
- His portfolio extended beyond Kanawana to include Camp Perrot
