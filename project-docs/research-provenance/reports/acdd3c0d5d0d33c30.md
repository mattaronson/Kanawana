### Search 2: "Mary Susannah Edgar" YWCA camp founder
- **Query:** `"Mary Susannah Edgar" YWCA camp founder`
- **Results:** 10 links returned
- **Key facts from snippets:**
  - Born May 23, 1889, Sundridge, Ontario; daughter of Joseph Edgar and Mary Little
  - Attended Havergal College, University of Ontario, YWCA National Training School (NYC)
  - 1912-1914: First National Girls' Work with YWCA in Canada
  - 1915-1919: Girls' Work Secretary in Montreal + Director of Camp Oolahwan
  - 1920: Purchased property on Lake Bernard near Sundridge
  - 1922: Opened Glen Bernard Camp with 38 campers; directed until 1956
  - Author of books, one-act plays, and hymns
  - Most famous hymn: "God Who Touchest Earth with Beauty" (won ACA prize 1925, published 1926, translated into several languages)
  - Died September 17, 1973
  - Archival fonds at Trent University (82-010) and Queen's University
