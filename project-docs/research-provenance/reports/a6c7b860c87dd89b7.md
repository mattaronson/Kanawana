## Research Report: Sean Day -- Camp YMCA Kanawana / YMCA Quebec

### Professional Titles (Confirmed)
- **Regional Director of Camps, YMCA Quebec** (also referred to as "YMCA Quebec" and "Quebec YMCAs")

He held dual YMCA titles simultaneously. All three roles appear in CBC and CTV sources from 2017 through 2022.

### Personal Background at Kanawana
- Sean Day **grew up at Camp Kanawana**. In the 2017 CBC article about the Stuart McLean fund, he stated: *"Having lived with him for that week up at camp and having myself grown up at the camp and having lived the impact myself, I guess for Stuart it was just a natural thing."*
- He has described the camp mission as *"helping kids to understand their potential in the context of community and nature."*
- No specific dates for his camper/counsellor years were found in public sources.

### Stuart McLean / Camp Kanawana Fund (2017)
- Stuart McLean (CBC Radio host of "The Vinyl Cafe") worked as a counsellor at Camp Kanawana for five summers starting in 1969.
- McLean returned to camp to host his show while Sean Day was director.
- After McLean's death, the **Stuart McLean Camp Kanawana Fund** was established to help kids in need attend camp.
- Sean Day was quoted extensively in the CBC article (Feb 2017).

Source: [Stuart McLean fund to help kids in need attend Quebec camp - CBC News](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/montreal/stuart-mclean-fund-to-help-kids-in-need-attend-quebec-camp-1.3987146)

### COVID-19 Pandemic Response (2020-2021)
**2020:**
- Quebec allowed only day camps to open. Kanawana could not run overnight operations.

**2021:**
- YMCA Quebec decided to keep Kanawana's overnight camp closed for a second consecutive summer.
- Sean Day said *"closing Camp YMCA Kanawana was not an easy decision"* but *"offering overnight services to more than 250 campers at once just didn't seem possible"* with Kanawana's facilities.
- Instead of overnight camp, YMCA Quebec pivoted to offering **10 different day camps** (up from 3 the previous summer).
- Quebec eventually gave sleepaway camps the green light in May 2021, but for Kanawana it came too late -- they stuck with the day camp decision.
- As QCA spokesperson, Day warned: *"There are many camps that are in danger of closing if they don't run and if they can't get government support."*

### 2022 Staffing Crisis
- Day said he is **usually fully staffed by end of April**, but as of late May 2022 had **only hired 75% of counsellors**.
- YMCA made **"huge increases" to salaries** that year to recruit staff, which "helped somewhat."
- Demand for spots was extremely high with **waitlists** across Quebec camps.
- Day called for more government support for nonprofits like the YMCA.
- He noted camps still needed to reassure potential staff it was safe to return after the pandemic.
