## Sean Day -- Research Findings

**Role and title:** Sean Day held the dual title of **Regional Director of Camps for the Quebec YMCAs** and **Director of Camp YMCA Kanawana** (Saint-Sauveur, Quebec). The existing KB (from oral history, Matt Aronson) dates his tenure as **2004 to approximately April 2023** (19 years).

**Personal connection:** Day **grew up at Camp Kanawana** himself. Matt Aronson's oral history also records that Sean Day was Aronson's counsellor during his camper years. In a 2017 CBC article about the Stuart McLean Fund, Day said: "Having myself grown up at the camp and having lived the impact myself... helping kids to understand their potential in the context of community and nature is what the camp is all about."

**Stuart McLean connection:** Day was director when Stuart McLean returned to Kanawana in 2005 to host a *Vinyl Cafe* show. He was quoted in the 2017 CBC article about the Stuart McLean Camp Kanawana Fund.

**Pandemic leadership (2021):** Day made the decision to close Camp Kanawana's overnight program during COVID-19, citing the impossibility of offering overnight services to 250+ campers. The YMCA pivoted to offering 10 day camps (up from 3 the previous summer).

**Staff shortages (2022):** In May 2022, Day reported Kanawana had only hired 75% of counsellors by end of April (normally fully staffed by then). The YMCA made "huge increases" to salaries. Waiting lists existed at many YMCA camps.

**Post-YMCA career:** After departing YMCA Quebec (c. April 2023), his LinkedIn profile (ca.linkedin.com/in/sean-day-13811132) shows the title **"Director of Fund Development"**. The original KB v3.3 identifies his current employer as the **Rossy Foundation**.

**Other:** He appeared in an anti-bullying video on the Camp YMCA Kanawana Facebook page. He participated in a Kanawana Town Hall (January 27, year uncertain) alongside Kate Taylor and Kevin Slezak.
