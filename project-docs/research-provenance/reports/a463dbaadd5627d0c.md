### Biographical Profile Constructed

**Edgar E. Smee** (b. c.1912-1913 Hamilton, Ontario; d. February 7, 2002, Glebe Centre, Ottawa, age 89)

- **Kanawana service**: Juvenile section director (1939, per McMorris thesis); Resident Director (1942, per Montreal Gazette Aug 5, 1942)
- **Hometown**: Hamilton, Ontario -- grew up swimming at Red Hill Creek
- **Left Hamilton c.1938** for a "30-year absence" (returned 1968). This timeline brackets his Kanawana service perfectly, strongly suggesting a Montreal-based YMCA career during those decades.
- **Post-YMCA career**: Returned to Hamilton 1968. Founded CHOP (Clear Hamilton of Pollution, 1969), later the Conserver Society of Hamilton & District (incorporated 1984). Helped create the first action plan for Hamilton Harbour/Burlington Bay.
- **Environmentalist of the Year, 1983** (EOY Awards)
- **Ed Smee Conserver Society Environmental Fund** established 1986, renamed in his honour 1999, managed by Hamilton Community Foundation
- **Family**: Wife Frieda; daughters Linda, Susan, Sydney, Sonia; brothers Norman and Leonard

### Key Inference

The "E.E." initials now resolve: **E**dgar **E**. Smee. The McMorris thesis uses "Edgar Smee" for 1939; the Gazette uses "E.E. Smee" for 1942. Same person.
