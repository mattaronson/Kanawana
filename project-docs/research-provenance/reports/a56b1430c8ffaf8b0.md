### 2. Personal Connection to Camp Kanawana

Sean Day grew up at Camp Kanawana. In the 2017 CBC article about the Stuart McLean Fund, he stated: **"Having lived with him for that week up at camp and having myself grown up at the camp and having lived the impact myself, I guess for Stuart it was just a natural thing."** This confirms he was a camper before becoming a staff member and eventually director.

### 3. Stuart McLean and the Stuart McLean Camp YMCA Kanawana Fund

- Stuart McLean worked at Camp Kanawana for five summers beginning in 1969, rising from counsellor to assistant director.
- McLean returned to Camp Kanawana in **2005** for a week and hosted one of his Vinyl Cafe CBC Radio shows there. Sean Day was director at the time.
- Before McLean's death from cancer on **February 15, 2017** (age 68), the McLean family established the **Stuart McLean Camp YMCA Kanawana Fund** to provide financial support for children and teens who cannot afford to attend camp.
- McLean's family requested donations to the fund in lieu of flowers.
- Sean Day's quotes about the fund: **"That was Stuart's experience, and he's so kindly asked people to support making sure that any kid who wants to experience that magic can."** He also said: **"Helping kids to understand their potential in the context of community and nature is what the camp is all about."**
- Context: In 2016, YMCA Quebec provided over $150,000 in financial support to children to attend Camp Kanawana, and the camp aimed for at least one in five campers to come from financially disadvantaged backgrounds.

### 4. COVID-19 Pandemic Leadership (2020-2022)

Sean Day was a prominent public voice for Quebec summer camps during the pandemic:

- **May 2020:** Said new public health rules requiring more staff and space meant some camps could not open at all. Called the guidelines "excellent" but noted they "make it a very tall order to be able to deliver safe camps."
- **March 2021:** As spokesperson for the Quebec Camping Association, said many camps were still trying to recover from financial losses. Stated: **"Everyone knows the COVID risk is serious and it has to be managed well, and to have the time to do that properly, we need clear guidelines."**
- **April 2021:** Announced YMCA Quebec would keep Camp Kanawana's overnight camp closed for summer 2021 due to pandemic uncertainty. Stated: **"More than ever, kids need to be able to play with one another, to get outside, to have a connection with one another and I think camp provides that opportunity."** YMCA Quebec pivoted to offering 10 different day camps (up from 3 the previous summer).
- **May 2021:** Even after Quebec gave sleepaway camps the green light, YMCA Quebec stuck with its closure decision. Day said offering overnight services to more than 250 campers at once was not feasible with Kanawana's facilities under pandemic conditions.
- **May 2022:** Reported post-pandemic staff shortages: **"I'm usually fully staffed by the end of April"** but had only hired 75% of counsellors by that date.
