### Camp Kanawana Connection (Confirmed)

Source: [Concordia University Archives - YMCA of Montreal fonds, sub-sub-series 12B01](https://www.concordia.ca/offices/archives/ymca-fonds-sub-sub-series-12B01.html)

### Publications (Confirmed)

### The SEVMS Legacy
The Special Event Volunteer Motivation Scale (SEVMS) that Twynam co-developed has become one of the foundational instruments in sport volunteerism research. It has been:
- Validated in Greek context (Grammatikopoulos, Koustelios, & Tsigilis, 2006, using YMCA children's summer camp volunteers)
- Validated in Chinese context (Cheung, Lam et al.)
- Cited alongside other major scales (VFI, OVMS, VMS-ISE) in international sport volunteering literature
- Twynam's works collectively have **723 citations** on ResearchGate
