### 1. CAMP SONGS AND CEREMONIES

**Kanawana Marching Song / Kanawana Cheer** (ALREADY DOCUMENTED in KB)
- The "Spirit of Kanawana" blog ([kanawana.blogspot.com](https://kanawana.blogspot.com/)) documents the history of the Kamp Kanawana Marching Song, which spells out "K-A-N-A-W-A-N-A, Kanawana!"
- The refrain "Hail, hail, the gang's all here" derives from the 1915 American song "Alabama Jubilee" (made famous by Fred Astaire), with lyrics by D. A. Esrom (pseudonym of Dolly Morse) set to a tune by Arthur Sullivan from *The Pirates of Penzance*.
- Lost verses were rediscovered in the Kanawana Archives at Concordia in 2006. The original was a three-verse song; only the first verse survives in the cheer known today.
- The blog author (Matt) has a theory involving the "Green Triangle" tradition about how the song arrived at Kamp.

**"On My Way to Kanawana" by Richard Kerr** (ALREADY DOCUMENTED as KB fact)
- No lyrics or recordings found online. The search for this exact title returned only the traditional gospel song "I'm On My Way to Canaan's Land."
- The project KB already records this song on CD archived at Concordia (P145/SR0001).
- Richard "Itche" Kerr is confirmed as the 2007 "Pip" Award recipient, described as "a volunteer working with the physically challenged." No additional biographical details found online beyond this.

**Emma Anders -- "Feels Like Home (Live)"** (NEW)
- Artist Emma Anders recorded a live track inspired by "the magic of Coyote Camp at YMCA Kanawana August 21st-25th, 2017."
- Released on [Bandcamp](https://emmaanders.bandcamp.com/track/feels-like-home-live).
- This confirms that music is still being created about/at the camp.

**Firelighting Ceremony** (NO SPECIFIC RESULTS for Kanawana)
- No online documentation of a specific Kanawana firelighting ceremony was found.
- General YMCA camp fire traditions documented include:
  - The **ash-continuity tradition** at Treasure Valley Family YMCA (Idaho): ashes from each closing campfire are collected and added to the next week's opening fire, creating a continuous chain.
  - The **Ragger Program** (originated 1914 by Thomas Caldwell at Oakland YMCA): campfire award ceremonies recognizing character traits.
  - YMCA's 125th camping anniversary in 2010 featured a nationwide simultaneous campfire event.

### 7. STUART McLEAN CONNECTION (COMPREHENSIVE)

This was the richest category. Multiple sources confirm and elaborate on the already-documented connection.

**Biography Summary (LARGELY ALREADY IN KB):**
- Born April 19, 1948, Montreal West; died February 15, 2017, Toronto, age 68.
- Grew up on Kensington Ave, Montreal West. "A small town upbringing in a big city."
- Educated at Lower Canada College and Bishop's College School. "Utter academic, athletic and social failure" -- repeated Grade 11.
- In summer 1969, visited a friend who worked at a Y camp and "never left" -- hired as a counsellor in the senior boys section.
- Served 5 summers (1969-1975): counsellor then assistant summer camp director (confirmed 1974-1975).
- "Camp is where I found my confidence." "The first place I ever felt I truly belonged."
- Took from camp "the accumulation of small moments" that changed his life.
