**Results:** Extensive across multiple searches

**Key biographical findings (confirmed against existing KB):**
- **Hedley Seldon Dimock (1891-1958):** Born February 11, 1891, South Rawdon, Hants, Nova Scotia. Parents: Charles Turner Torry Dimock and Catherine E. Dimock. Married Elizabeth Marguerite Gardiner, 2 children. WWI veteran (PPCLI), severely wounded August 1918 (grenade). Dean of YMCA College/George Williams College, Chicago. Launched character education institutes 1930. Co-authored "Camping and Character" (1929/1931) with Charles E. Hendry from Camp Ahmek research (1923). Also co-authored "Talks to Counselors" (1939) with Taylor Statten. Director of Training for USO during WWII. Died October 4, 1958 in Burlingame, California.
- **Hedley Gardner Dimock (born 1928):** Son of H.S. Dimock (circumstantial evidence: middle name "Gardner" matches wife's maiden name "Gardiner"). BA Beloit College; MA and EdD Columbia. Coordinator of Staff Development and Training for Montreal YMCA (1961). Conducted camp counselor research at Kanawana (1960-1970). First Chairman, Dept. of Applied Social Science, Sir George Williams University (1963). Director, Centre for Human Relations and Community Studies. Retired from Concordia 1980. Later Director of Centre for Human Resource Development, Puslinch, Ontario; adjunct professor at University of Guelph. Published extensively on group dynamics.
- **ACA Hedley S. Dimock Award** established 1965. First recipients (1965): Julian W. Smith, Sigurd F. Olson, Stewart L. Udall. 2026 recipient: John Hamilton.
