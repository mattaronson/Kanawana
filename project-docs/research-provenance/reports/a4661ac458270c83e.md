### The SEVMS (Special Event Volunteer Motivation Scale)

The SEVMS is Twynam's most impactful scholarly contribution. Key details:
- **28-item scale** adapted from Cnaan and Goldberg-Glen (1991)
- **Four factors:** purposive, solidary, external traditions, commitments
- Key finding: the highest-ranking reason for volunteering was "I wanted to help make the event a success"
- Has been validated internationally:
  - **Greek validation** (Grammatikopoulos, Koustelios, & Tsigilis, 2006) -- tested on 272 YMCA camp volunteers
  - **Chinese validation** (Cheung, Xie, & Lam, 2012)
  - Widely cited across sport event management, leisure studies, and volunteer research
