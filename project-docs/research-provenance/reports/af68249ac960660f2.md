## Camp Kanawana Social Media & Alumni Research -- Summary

**Founded 1894** by "Billy" Ball of YMCA Montreal. Originally called Camp Jubilee on Lake St. Joseph near Sainte-Agathe with 20 campers; later moved to current 550-acre Saint-Sauveur site with 3 lakes.

### Notable Alumni (via the "Pip" Award)

The annual alumni award, named for Philip "Pip" Caddell (camper 1928) and his grandson James Caddell (camper/counsellor 1980s-90s), has recognized:

- **Stuart McLean** (2009) -- CBC Vinyl Cafe host; 5 summers as counsellor/assistant director starting 1969. His family established [The Stuart McLean Camp Kanawana Fund](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/montreal/stuart-mclean-fund-to-help-kids-in-need-attend-quebec-camp-1.3987146) after his death.
- **John Cleghorn** (2012) -- Former CEO, Royal Bank of Canada.
- **Terry "Aislin" Mosher** (2015) -- Montreal Gazette editorial cartoonist.
- **Dr. James Orbinski** (2024) -- Doctors Without Borders humanitarian.
- **Dr. Marina Sharpe** (2018) -- Refugee law expert.
- **Rick Patten** (2008) -- Former Ontario cabinet minister.
