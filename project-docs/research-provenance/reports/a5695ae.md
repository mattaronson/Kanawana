**JAMES CADDELL BIOGRAPHY (SIGNIFICANT NEW DETAIL):**
- Full name: James Lawrence Caddell
- Born: March 16, 1973, Montreal
- Education: Cortland High School near Syracuse, NY (graduated 1991); Dawson College; McGill University (BA 1997); Royal Military College Kingston (MA in War Studies)
- Military: Reserve unit of Royal Canadian Hussars; tour in Bosnia as UN peacekeeper; awarded Peacekeeping Service and UNPROFOR medals
- 1997 Normandy Foundation Scholar (6-week battlefield tour)
- Intern at Pearson Peacekeeping Centre, Nova Scotia
- Lived with brother Chris in Los Angeles briefly
- Married Tara Newell (BA'98 McGill, MA Queen's/RMC in public administration); they met at Queen's/RMC Kingston
- Both worked for federal government in Ottawa
- Left government jobs September 2004 for round-the-world backpacking trip
- CMAT volunteer: needs assessment in tsunami-devastated Kanniyakumari district, Tamil Nadu, India (Feb 2005); 2 months with children orphaned by tsunamis
- Also humanitarian work in East Timor and Indonesia
- Death: October 16, 2005, Tupiza, Bolivia -- pulmonary edema caused by altitude sickness; had previously suffered altitude sickness on Mt. Everest above 5,000m; Tupiza below 3,000m; no oxygen available even at local hospital
- Survived by wife Tara Newell, mother Norma Lewis, father Andrew Caddell, and seven half-siblings
- Legacy: James Caddell Memorial Scholarship for Orphans (funds impoverished children in South India)
- Source: CMAT website (Globe and Mail obituary by Sandra Martin, Oct 22, 2005)

**SELECTION CRITERIA (PRECISE WORDING FOUND):**
From the Marina Sharpe 2018 and Chris Adam 2017 YMCA pages:
> "The Pip" is awarded to the person who, in his or her life, best exemplifies the values of Non Nobis Solum -- Kanawana's motto, meaning "Not For Ourselves Alone," which represents the strong spirit of cooperation and dedication to the community.

> More specifically, "The Pip" is awarded to the candidate who has taken that philosophy out into the community and the world and made a contribution to the welfare of others, after being a camper, CIT/LIT, or staff member at Kanawana.

Andrew Caddell quote: "The purpose of this award is to recognize people who have taken the values they learned at Kanawana and applied them to the larger world."
