**Pagé Family of Saint-Sauveur-des-Monts -- Research Summary**

**Founding generation.** Magloire Pagé (1826-1901), a 10th-generation descendant of the original Pagé family who came to New France, married Adéline Beauchamp (1830-1898) on July 28, 1851 in Saint-Jérôme. By the 1885 census they owned **lot 43, concession of côte Saint-Elmire**, Saint-Sauveur.

**Second generation.** Their son, also named **Magloire Pagé fils** (b. 1853, Saint-Sauveur), acquired the paternal land in 1874 and married **Olivine Desjardins** on August 3, 1875. Olivine died at 54 on June 20, 1904 (buried in Saint-Sauveur cemetery). Known children:
- **Léonie Pagé** (1882-1941), married Joseph-Vital Léonard in 1902
- **Édouard Pagé** (b. March 30, 1893)
- **Télesphore Pagé** (youngest son, married May 27, 1925)

**Third generation -- Édouard.** Apprenticed in baking in 1910 under Ubald Gauthier on Drolet Street, Montreal. In 1912, Camille Gauvreau (who founded the first Saint-Sauveur bakery in **1897**, not 1903) ceded his shop to his son Ovila, who rented it to Édouard. Pagé acquired the business in **1914**. He later served as president of the Saint-Sauveur School Commission and secured government authorization for a boys' school -- **École Saint-Édouard**, blessed January 9, 1949, named in his honor (name retained until 1995). In **1963** he sold the bakery to his sons **Bernard and Philippe Pagé**. The bakery (7 avenue de l'Église) still operates as Boulangerie Pagé / Les Moulins La Fayette.

**Third generation -- Télesphore.** Inherited the family land and settled with his wife **Clarilda Dagenais** (1901-1995). The old Pagé farmhouse "faced the mountains."

**Mont Habitant connection.** In **1957**, **Mickey Stein** was staying in the old Pagé family farmhouse when he observed the mountain opposite and conceived Mont Habitant ski resort (opened 1958-59 season).

**YMCA connection -- gap.** No direct documentation found for the **1910 land sale/donation** to the YMCA or the **1960 "Pagé farm" purchase** terms. These transactions remain documented only in the McMorris thesis and YMCA sources already in the KB. The Pagé family held extensive land in the area (lot 43, côte Saint-Elmire; the Domaine-Pagé locality; the farmhouse), but which specific parcel(s) went to the YMCA and under what terms is still undetermined.

**Domaine-Pagé** is a named locality in Saint-Sauveur (with streets "Chemin du Domaine-Pagé" and "1re rue du Domaine-Pagé"), but the Quebec Commission de toponymie states the origin has **not been formally determined**.

**KB updates:** 8 new facts (f_1226 through f_1233) and 6 new sources added. The "1903" founding date for the bakery may be inaccurate -- Gauvreau established it in 1897; Pagé took it over 1912-1914. This is flagged as a potential conflict with existing fact f_0843.
