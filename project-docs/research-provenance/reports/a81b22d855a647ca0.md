### 2. KEY PERSONNEL

| Person | Role | Dates |
|--------|------|-------|
| **Mary Susanna Edgar** (1889-1973) | Founder; also YWCA Girls' Work Secretary, Montreal | 1915-1919 |
| **Ferna G. Halliday** | Secretary of YWCA Montreal, responsible for girls at Oolahwan | 1918-1921 |
| **Gail Small** | Director of Camp Oolahwan | Unknown dates (later career) |

Edgar left Oolahwan in 1919, spent 1920 in Japan doing YWCA volunteer work, then purchased property on Lake Bernard in Ontario and founded Glen Bernard Camp in 1922. Halliday left in 1921 and co-founded Camp Ouareau with Mabel Jamieson. Edgar also authored several books including "Wood-fire and Candlelight" and the hymn "God Who Touchest Earth with Beauty," and served as first CCA vice-president (1936).

### 3. CONNECTIONS TO KANAWANA

- **1946 cocoa powder incident**: Kanawana campers were covered in cocoa powder to greet visitors from Camp Oolahwan as "Indians" (KB facts f_0220, f_0718).
- **YMCA/YWCA personnel network**: Both camps operated under the Montreal Y umbrella.
- **Geographic proximity**: Both in the Laurentians north of Montreal, facilitating inter-camp visits.

Beyond the 1946 episode, no other specific Kanawana-Oolahwan interactions have been documented yet.

### 4. CONNECTIONS TO OTHER CAMPS

- **Camp Ouareau**: Halliday went from Oolahwan to co-found Ouareau (1922). Ouareau hosted the Oolahwan centennial reunion in September 2017.
- **Glen Bernard Camp**: Edgar founded it in 1922 after leaving Oolahwan.
- **Camp Oconto**: Halliday founded it (1924) after Ouareau.
