  - Earliest documented reference: 1923 *Gas Bag* (14th season chronicle) listing "Lac Wilson" as a hike destination

    3. Reference to J.W. McConnell's own middle name "Wilson" (his mother's maiden name)

**2. J.W. McConnell Article**
- **File:** `/home/user/Kanawana/wiki/people/j-w-mcconnell.md`
- **Last Updated:** 2026-06-13
- **Key Findings:**
  - John Wilson McConnell (1877–1963) was Montreal financier and YMCA benefactor
  - His full name: John **Wilson** McConnell (middle name from mother's maiden name)
  - Had four children, all given the middle name Griffith; eldest son was **Wilson Griffith McConnell** (1908–1966)
  - McConnell was deeply connected to YMCA: rented a room at YMCA in 1901; led 1909 fundraising campaign that raised $320,000
  - **Critical Discovery (2026-06-13):** Oral history from Matt Aronson reveals "Lac Desjardins was the original name of Lake Wilson, renamed after McConnell donation"
  - The lake naming to McConnell is "plausible but unverified" — no documentary evidence found

**3. The Desjardins Connection & Pagé Family**
- **File:** `/home/user/Kanawana/wiki/people/page-family.md`
- **Last Updated:** 2026-06-13
- **Critical Discovery:** Magloire Pagé fils married **Olivine Desjardins** on August 3, 1875
  - This genealogical connection explains the "Lac Desjardins" original name pattern
  - Quebec lakes commonly named for landowner families; if the lake sat on Pagé-Desjardins land, "Lac Desjardins" would refer to the wife's maiden family
  - The Pagé family owned lot 43 on côte Saint-Elmire in Saint-Sauveur and sold property to the YMCA circa 1910
