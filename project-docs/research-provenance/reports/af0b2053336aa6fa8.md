## Search 1: "Montreal Brewing Company" history Cushing

**Exact query:** `"Montreal Brewing Company" history Cushing`

**Findings:**
- The Montreal Brewing Company was one of ten breweries operating in Greater Montreal in 1898 (alongside Molson, Dow, Dawes, Ekers, Union, de la Court, Reinhardt, Star, and Canadian Brewing).
- **Hubert Cushing** was a Montreal Brewing Company executive who became a minority owner of the Montreal Royals baseball team. In January 1908, a $10,000 (Canadian) deal was brokered for three Montreal business owners to purchase the Royals: majority owner Sam Lichtenhein, plus minority owners Hubert Cushing and E.R. Carrington.
- **Thomas Cushing** founded the Montreal Brewing Company in 1876. He was the son of Lemuel Cushing of Trois-Rivieres/Chatham, from a United Empire Loyalist family. The brewery was "one of the smaller ones in Quebec." Its products included India Pale Ale, pale ale, stout porter, and the distinctive "sand porter" (advertised 1882-1892, disappeared by 1900).
- In 1909, Montreal Brewing merged with Dawes Brewery and others to form **National Breweries Limited**.
- **Gordon Cushing** (son of Thomas) served on the National Breweries board and later became a stockbroker. He "stayed on the board of National until the end." Thomas had two sons associated with National Breweries.

**Sources:** [SABR - Montreal Royals ownership](https://sabr.org/bioproj/topic/montreal-royals-team-ownership-history/), [Beer Et Seq - Sand Porter Part I](https://www.beeretseq.com/the-sand-porter-of-montreal-part-i/), [Beer Et Seq - Ten Breweries 1898](https://www.beeretseq.com/the-ten-breweries-of-montreal-in-1898/)

## Search 4: "Lemuel Cushing" Quebec judge OR politician

**Exact query:** `"Lemuel Cushing" Quebec judge OR politician`

**Findings -- two distinct Lemuel Cushings and one Thomas Cushing Aylwin:**

### Lemuel Cushing Sr. (1806-1875)
- Born April 29, 1806 in Trois-Rivieres, Lower Canada. Eighth child of **Job Cushing** and **Sarah (Sally) Rice**, a United Empire Loyalist family descended from Matthew Cushing who arrived in Hingham, Massachusetts in 1638.
- Married **Catherine Hutchins** of Lachute on August 25, 1836.
- **Thirteen children**, including **eight sons**, all "active and powerful businessmen."
- Businessman of Chatham, Quebec. Built wealth through the Ottawa Valley timber trade.
- Mayor of Chatham Township, 1861-1872.
- Warden of Argenteuil County, 1872-1875.
- Volunteer militiaman during the 1837 insurrection.
- Died May 18, 1875 in Montreal. Buried in Chatham.
- The hamlet of **Cushing, Quebec** (now part of Brownsburg-Chatham) is named after him.

### Lemuel Cushing Jr. (1842-1881)
- Born in Chatham, Canada East, son of Lemuel Cushing Sr. and Catherine Hutchins.
- Educated at McGill University; called to the Lower Canada bar in 1865.
- **President of the Montreal YMCA, 1869-1870** -- the first documented Cushing-YMCA link.
- Represented Argenteuil as Liberal MP in the House of Commons, 1874-1875 (by-election set aside for irregularities).
- Published *The Genealogy of the Cushing Family* (1877).
- Married **Ellen Myra Macdougall** in 1869.
- Died March 1, 1881, age 39.
- Four children: **Walter Edgar** (b. 1870), **Harold Beveridge** (b. 1873), **Florence Ethelwyn** (b. 1874), **James Stevenson** (b. 1878).

### Thomas Cushing Aylwin (1806-1871) -- SEPARATE FAMILY
- Born January 5, 1806 in Quebec City. His **grandmother was Lucy Cushing** (who moved from Massachusetts to Quebec after the American Revolution), making his middle name "Cushing" a maternal family reference.
- He was NOT from the same Cushing line as the Chatham/Argenteuil family. Different branch.
- Career: lawyer, politician, judge. Called to bar 1827. Served as Solicitor General for Canada East. Appointed to Court of Queen's Bench 1848.
- Married Margaret Nelson Hanna (1832); after her death, married Eliza Margaret Felton (1836).
- Died October 14, 1871 in Montreal.

**Sources:** [DCB - Cushing, Lemuel](https://www.biographi.ca/en/bio/cushing_lemuel_10E.html), [Wikipedia - Lemuel Cushing Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lemuel_Cushing,_Jr.), [DCB - Aylwin, Thomas Cushing](http://www.biographi.ca/en/bio/aylwin_thomas_cushing_10E.html), [Geni - Lemuel Cushing](https://www.geni.com/people/Lemuel-Cushing/6000000008369908038)

## Search 5: "Cushing" YMCA Montreal camping historical

**Exact query:** `"Cushing" YMCA Montreal camping historical`

**Findings:**

### The 1892 Camping Pioneer
- In 1892, **"Lemuel Cushing"** brought the first group of boys to Lake Saint-Joseph in Saint-Adolphe-d'Howard, inaugurating the Montreal YMCA's summer camping program. This is per the QAHN article.
- In 1894, Camp Jubilee (named for the YMCA's 50th anniversary) formally opened as Quebec's first residential summer camp, with 20 campers.
- **Identity puzzle:** Lemuel Jr. died in 1881, so the 1892 "Lemuel Cushing" cannot be him. The existing project wiki (already researched in depth) identifies only one literal "Lemuel Cushing" alive in Montreal in 1892: Lemuel (b. November 26, 1875), son of James Brock Cushing -- but he was only 16. The leading hypothesis is that the trip was actually led by **Walter Edgar Cushing** (b. 1870, age 22 in 1892), Lemuel Jr.'s eldest son, and the "Lemuel Cushing" attribution is a conflation of the family name.

### W.E. Cushing (almost certainly Walter Edgar Cushing)
- Authored two documents in 1943: "Historical sketches -- Lake St. Joseph" and "Early Days at Lake St. Joseph," preserved in the Concordia University Archives (YMCA of Montreal fonds P145, sub-series 12A).
- Also had correspondence regarding the history of East End YMCA work (1906-1933).
- The 1905 Cushing genealogy has no "William E." Cushing anywhere in the Montreal branch. The ONLY person matching the initials W.E. is **Walter Edgar Cushing** (b. 1870), who would have been 73 in 1943.
- If W.E. Cushing = Walter Edgar Cushing, then the same man who may have led the 1892 trip later wrote the historical account of it in 1943.

### Dr. Cushing (1946)
- A "Dr. Cushing" advised keeping Camp Kanawana open during the 1946 polio outbreak. The project's existing research identifies this as almost certainly **Harold Beveridge Cushing** (b. 1873), Lemuel Jr.'s second son, who graduated McGill M.D. in 1898 and was a physician at the Royal Victoria Hospital/Children's Hospital -- a pediatrician, exactly matching the polio context.

### John Crosbie Cushing
- Mayor of Westmount (in office ~1957-1960s). In 1959, he presented Queen Elizabeth II and Prince Philip with a silver maple syrup jug during their royal visit. Westmount still sends annual maple syrup to Buckingham Palace.
- Relationship to the Chatham/Argenteuil Cushing family is not confirmed by my searches but highly likely given the Montreal milieu and the distinctive middle name "Crosbie."

**Sources:** [QAHN - YMCA Camp of Saint-Adolphe d'Howard](https://qahn.org/article/ymca-camp-saint-adolphe-dhoward), [Concordia Archives - YMCA fonds sub-series 12A](https://www.concordia.ca/offices/archives/ymca-fonds-sub-series-12A.html), [CORE - McMorris thesis](https://core.ac.uk/outputs/591919397/)
