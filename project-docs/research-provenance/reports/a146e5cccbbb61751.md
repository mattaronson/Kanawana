## IV. ORAL HISTORY INSTRUMENT QUEUE (from project-docs/oral-history-instrument.md)

### Section 1: The Chopsy Legend
- Tell the story as you remember hearing it (or telling it)
- How is the name spelled/pronounced?
- When and where was it told (Council Ring, cabin at lights-out, canoe trips, a specific campsite)?
- Was there a jump-scare, a ritual element, or a counsellor "performance"?

- Did the story resemble the American "Cropsey" pattern (a disfigured man with an axe/hook)?

### Section 2: Songs, Cheers, and Grace
- Sing/write out the Marching Song as sung in your era — every verse you can recall

- Were there cheers/chants tied to specific events — canoe trip departures/returns, banquet, L&V games, the dam ceremony?

### Section 3: Grand Portage, the Longhouse, and Building Names
- What and where was/is **Grand Portage**?
- What and where was/is the **Longhouse**?
- List every **named building, cabin, or place** you can remember
- Which buildings existed in your era that are now gone?

### Section 4: Lake Desjardins

- What stories attached to it?

### Section 6: Directors, Staff, and Eras

### Section 7: Traditions and Ceremonies
