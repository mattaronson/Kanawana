## 1. James Orbinski (2024 Pip Award Recipient)

**Full name:** James Jude Orbinski
**Born:** 1960, England
**Education:** Dawson CEGEP (Montreal); BA Psychology, Trent University (1984); MD, McMaster University (1990); MA International Relations, University of Toronto Munk Centre (1998)
**Kanawana connection:** Camper and counsellor in the 1980s and 1990s. Grew up in the Notre-Dame-de-Grace neighbourhood of Montreal after his Irish-born parents immigrated to Canada when he was seven.
**Career highlights:**
- Began working with MSF in 1991; served in Somalia (1992-93), Afghanistan (1994), Rwanda during genocide (1994), and Goma/Zaire (1996-97)
- Elected President of MSF International Council in 1998
- Accepted the 1999 Nobel Peace Prize on behalf of MSF in Oslo; used the speech to condemn Russian violence in Chechnya; directed prize money to MSF's Campaign for Access to Essential Medicines
- Awarded Canada's Meritorious Service Cross (1997) for Rwanda service
- Officer of the Order of Canada (2009)
- Author of *An Imperfect Offering: Humanitarian Action for the 21st Century* (2008), winner of the Shaughnessy Cohen Award
- Subject of documentary *Triage: Dr. James Orbinski's Humanitarian Dilemma*
- Currently Principal of Massey College, University of Toronto (2024-25), and Full Professor in the Department of Family and Community Medicine, Temerty Faculty of Medicine
**Why he received the award:** His extraordinary humanitarian career embodies the Kanawana motto *Non Nobis Solum* ("Not For Ourselves Alone").

Sources: [Wikipedia - James Orbinski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Orbinski), [MSF Nobel Peace Prize](https://www.doctorswithoutborders.org/who-we-are/our-history/nobel-peace-prize-25-years), [U of T Magazine profile](https://magazine.utoronto.ca/research-ideas/culture-society/james-orbinski-profile-doctors-without-borders-canadians-in-rwanda/), [YMCA Quebec announcement](https://www.ymcaquebec.org/en/about-us/newsroom/2024/dr-james-orbinski-receives-the-camp-ymca-kanawana-pip-award), [Concordia notable alumni](https://www.concordia.ca/content/shared/en/profiles/offices/advancement/notable-concordians/james-orbinski.html)

## 5. The Pip Alumni Award

**Established:** 2007
**Sponsor:** Andrew Caddell (son of Philip "Pip" Caddell, father of James Caddell)
**Named for:** Philip "Pip" Caddell (1913-2004) and James Caddell (1973-2005)
**Selection committee:** Composed of Kanawana alumni
**Criteria:** Awarded to the person who best exemplifies the values of *Non Nobis Solum* -- Kanawana's motto meaning "Not For Ourselves Alone." The recipient must be a former camper, CIT/LIT, or staff member (full-time or summer) at Kanawana who has taken that philosophy into the community and the world and made a contribution to the welfare of others.

**Complete known recipient list:**
- 2007: Richard "Itche" Kerr
- 2008: Rick Patten
- 2009: Stuart McLean
- 2010: (no record found; possibly not awarded)
- 2011: Bruce Netherwood
- 2012: John Cleghorn
- 2013: Sam Lazarus (posthumously) and the Lazarus family
- 2014: Jeniene Phillips Birks
- 2015: Terry "Aislin" Mosher
- 2016: Carol Skinner
- 2017: Chris Adam
- 2018: Marina Sharpe
- 2019-2023: Not confirmed online (Andrew Caddell's LinkedIn truncates at "2019 Dr...")
- 2024: Dr. James Orbinski

Sources: [YMCA Quebec - Carol Skinner](https://www.ymcaquebec.org/en/news/als-advocate-carol-skinner-receives-camp-ymca-kanawana), [YMCA Quebec - James Orbinski](https://www.ymcaquebec.org/en/about-us/newsroom/2024/dr-james-orbinski-receives-the-camp-ymca-kanawana-pip-award), [YMCA Quebec - Terry Mosher](https://www.ymcaquebec.org/en/About-Us/Newsroom/2015-en/Terry-Aislin-Mosher-Receives-YMCA-Kanawana-Pip)

## 6. Philip "Pip" Caddell (1913-2004) and James Caddell (1973-2005)

### Philip "Pip" Caddell (1913-2004)
- Attended Camp Kanawana as a camper in 1928
- Officer in the Canadian Army abroad during WWII (1939-1945)
- Montreal executive
- Valued volunteer in numerous community organizations in Montreal
- One of his spoken stories was transcribed by his son Andrew Caddell and published in the book *The Goal* (co-authored by Andrew Caddell and Dave Stubbs)
- No independent obituary or detailed biographical record was found online

### James Lawrence Caddell (1973-2005)
- Camper and counsellor at Kanawana, 1983-1991
- Attended Dawson CEGEP and McGill University in Montreal
- Joined the Royal Canadian Hussars regiment in Montreal while at Dawson and McGill
- Served a UN peacekeeping tour of duty in Bosnia (1995)
- Normandy Battlefields Scholar
- Intern at Pearson Peacekeeping Institute, Nova Scotia
- Master's degree from Royal Military College, Kingston
- Senior Program Manager with Human Resources Development Canada
- Member of 2nd Intelligence Platoon, Ottawa; received the Queen's Jubilee Medal
- Died October 16, 2005, in Tupiza, Bolivia, of pulmonary edema caused by altitude sickness at age 32, while on a round-the-world trip with his wife Tara Newell
- During their travels (beginning June 2004), they assisted in humanitarian work in East Timor, Indonesia, and a tsunami orphanage in India; trekked in Nepal; toured Australia, Vietnam, Cambodia, Gallipoli, Jerusalem, and Provence; studied Spanish in Buenos Aires and Santiago
- The James Caddell Memorial Scholarship for Orphans was established in his name, funding education for orphaned and impoverished children in South India

### Andrew Caddell (award sponsor)
- Son of Philip, father of James
- Retired from Global Affairs Canada in 2017 after 16 years
- Weekly columnist for the Hill Times (Ottawa)
- Town Councillor in Kamouraska, Quebec
- Lectures part-time at the Max Bell School of Public Policy, McGill University
- Serves as Quebec negotiator with the Mohawk community of Kahnawake
- Co-authored *The Goal* with Dave Stubbs
