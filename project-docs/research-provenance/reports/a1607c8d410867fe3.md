### Camp Weredale
- Founded **1934** by the **Rotary Club of Westmount**, which bought 260 acres on **Lac l'Achigan** (Saint-Hippolyte, QC) and gave the property to **Weredale House** (the Boys' Home of Montreal, founded 1870 by Charles Alexander).
- Suffered a fire and was rebuilt in **1965**.
- In **1977**, the YMCA of Montreal proposed a **two-site operation involving Kanawana and Weredale**. Concordia Archives (YMCA fonds Sub-series 12A) hold situation reports from 1977-1983 that profile Kanawana, Camp Otoreke, and Camp Weredale together.
- Weredale House merged into Youth Horizons (now Batshaw) in 1977. Camp Weredale continues to operate independently under the Weredale Foundation.

### Camp Ouareau
- Founded **1922** by **Mabel Jamieson** and **Ferna Halliday** as an all-girls camp at Notre-Dame-de-la-Merci on Lac Ouareau.
- **Not a YMCA camp**, but Jamieson was **secretary general of the YMCA of Montreal**, providing a personnel link to the Kanawana orbit.
- YWCA **Camp Oolahwan** (founded 1917) held its centennial reunion at Camp Ouareau in 2017.

### Camp Otoreke / Camp Jubilee
- In **1892**, **Lemuel Cushing** of the Montreal YMCA brought boys to Lake Saint-Joseph in Saint-Adolphe-d'Howard, inaugurating the YMCA camping program.
- **Camp Jubilee** was named for the YMCA's 50th anniversary (1893/1894), located on islands in Lake St. Joseph.
- Renamed **Camp Otoreke** ("home in the north") in **1909**; operated until **1982**.
- Concordia Archives Sub-series 12L holds Camp Jubilee records from 1893-1902 (fishing rights, photos, brochures, land purchase correspondence).

### Canadian YMCA Camp Network
- **Big Cove YMCA Camp** (Merigomish, NS, **1889**): Canada's oldest residential camp. Kanawana (1894) is second oldest nationally, oldest in Quebec.
- **Camp Pine Crest** (Torrance, ON, **1910**): YMCA of Greater Toronto. No Quebec Pine Crest found.
- **John Island Camp** (Lake Huron, ON, **1954**): YMCA of Northeastern Ontario.

### Camping Associations
- The **Canadian Camping Association** was founded **May 20, 1936** at the Central YMCA in Toronto. Taylor Statten was first president; Mary Edgar was first vice-president.
- The **Ontario Camping Association** archives at Trent University hold a **Kanawana file dated 1940**.
- Kanawana is currently listed with the **Association des camps du Quebec**.
- Camp motto confirmed: **"Non Nobis Solum"** ("Not for ourselves alone").

### Stuart McLean
- Counsellor at Kanawana starting **1969**, five summers, became **assistant summer camp director**.
- Called Kanawana "the first place I ever felt I truly belonged." Once passed up Carleton University journalism program to return to camp.
- After his death **February 2017**, his family established the **Stuart McLean Camp Kanawana Fund**.
