### 1. BAnQ (Bibliotheque et Archives nationales du Quebec)

**Result: Limited direct hits, but two key leads confirmed.**

- **BAnQ Advitam portal** ([advitam.banq.qc.ca](https://advitam.banq.qc.ca/)) -- The archival search interface for Quebec's largest archive collection. A search for "YMCA" returns at least one photographic record: ["Swimming. Verdun Y.M.C.A"](https://advitam.banq.qc.ca/notice/548244). No Kanawana-specific record surfaced via web search, but the internal search tool may yield more results.

- **BAnQ Advitam Notice 519920** (ALREADY TRACKED as `src_banq_advitam_519920`) -- References "Les archives du YMCA de Montreal, 1985" within the Fonds Archives nationales du Quebec. Indicates BAnQ received/processed YMCA Montreal archival materials in 1985. Relationship to the Concordia holdings is unclear.

- **BAnQ Advitam Notice 315906** (ALREADY TRACKED as `src_banq_advitam_315906`) -- Contains "YMCA annual Drama festival & St Andrew's church postcard, 1937." Relates to YMCA de Quebec City, not Montreal.

- **BAnQ numerique** ([numerique.banq.qc.ca](https://numerique.banq.qc.ca/)) -- No Kanawana-specific results surfaced via external web search. One tangential hit: a 1970 issue of *Il cittadino canadese* and the Lovell's Montreal Directory 1944 appeared but with no confirmed Kanawana content. **The BAnQ digitized newspaper collection (including Montreal Gazette, La Presse, Le Devoir) would need to be searched directly through their internal platform.**

**Assessment:** BAnQ is a potentially rich but under-explored resource. The internal search tools likely contain digitized newspaper references that do not appear in external search engines.

### 2. Quebec Newspapers Digitized

**Result: Multiple confirmed historical newspaper references, several new leads.**

#### Montreal Gazette (via Newspapers.com)

- **The Gazette, July 7, 1897** (ALREADY TRACKED as `src_newspapers_gazette_1897`) -- ["YMCA Summer Camp Kanawana"](https://www.newspapers.com/article/the-gazette-ymca-summer-camp-kanawana/35176458/). One of the earliest known newspaper references. Behind paywall.

- **The Gazette, July 7, 1913** (NEW) -- [Page 3](https://www.newspapers.com/newspage/419795762/). Two detachments of Montreal Y.M.C.A. boys left for Kamp Kanawana. Programme adapted for specific ages; instruction in athletics, educational subjects, and Bible study. Speakers included Mr. M. F. Furey (physical director) and Dr. Hamilton. **Not yet in sources.json.**

- **The Gazette, July 11, 1918** (ALREADY TRACKED as `src_newspapers_gazette_1918`) -- [Page 9](https://www.newspapers.com/newspage/419701340/). Largest attendance of any season (110 members one weekend). Activities included first aid, basket-making, wrestling, nature study. Visited by Frank Lawes of London filming YMCA work worldwide.

- **The Gazette, July 27, 1918** (ALREADY TRACKED as `src_newspapers_gazette_1918b`) -- "Youngsters Have Gay Time at Camp - Kanawana." Behind paywall.

- **The Gazette, January 24, 2004** (NEW) -- [Obituary page](https://www.newspapers.com/newspage/425779783/). Donations requested to the Samuel Lazarus Fund, Kamp Kanawana, 1435 Drummond Street, 4th Floor, Montreal. **Not yet in sources.json.** This reference is notable for confirming the Drummond Street address and the existence of a dedicated fund (Samuel Lazarus Fund).

#### La Presse

- **La Presse, May 15, 2018** (ALREADY TRACKED as `src_lapresse_ymca_2018`) -- "Un million pour les YMCA." Desjardins $1M donation. Confirms 1894 founding.

- No historical (pre-2000) La Presse or Le Devoir articles surfaced via web search. These newspapers' digitized archives would need direct BAnQ platform searching.

#### Other Newspaper Leads

- The Concordia AtoM finding aid notes "tear sheets from local newspapers with advertisements for Kamp Kanawana" dating from 1993-1994, and "newspaper articles on camping from 1947" within the camping associations sub-series.

### 3. Canadian Heritage Databases

#### Library and Archives Canada (bac-lac.gc.ca)

- **No direct Kamp Kanawana results** in their indexed collections via web search. However, LAC holds YMCA national-level records. The search indicates that until 1912, Canadian YMCAs were jointly administered by the International Committee of the YMCA of North America.

- **University of Minnesota Kautz Family YMCA Archives** (NEW lead) -- [Collection page](https://archives.lib.umn.edu/repositories/7/resources/992). Contains "YMCA in Canada records." Since pre-1912 Canadian YMCA records were administered internationally, this US-based archive may contain early Montreal YMCA / Kanawana material. **Not yet in sources.json.**

#### Canadian Geographical Names Database

- **Camp Kanawana** is an official place name in Quebec. [GNBC entry ID: EKWXQ](https://geonames.nrcan.gc.ca/search-place-names/unique?id=EKWXQ). Maintained by Natural Resources Canada.

#### The Canadian Encyclopedia / Historica Canada

- **No dedicated article on Kamp Kanawana** exists in The Canadian Encyclopedia. The general ["Camping" article](https://www.thecanadianencyclopedia.ca/en/article/camping) mentions YMCA camping beginning in the 1890s but does not name Kanawana specifically.

#### Canadian Centre for Architecture (CCA)

- **Doctor's Cottage for Kamp Kanawana** -- [Ross & Macdonald fonds](https://www.cca.qc.ca/en/archives/45474/fonds-ross-macdonald/387039/projects/50576/doctors-cottage-for-kamp-kanawana). Architectural drawings for a doctor's cottage at Kamp Kanawana, designed by the prominent Montreal firm Ross & Macdonald (who also designed the Mount Royal Hotel, Chateau Laurier, and Union Station Toronto). **Not yet in sources.json.** This is a significant new source confirming that a major architectural firm designed buildings for the camp.

### 4. Concordia University Pages

**Result: The richest source category. Confirms and expands existing knowledge.**

#### YMCA of Montreal Fonds (P0145)

- **Fonds overview** (ALREADY TRACKED as `src_concordia_atom_fonds_p0145`) -- [AtoM portal](https://concordia.accesstomemory.org/ymca-of-montreal-fonds-2). Reference code P0145. Donated to Concordia 2000-2005 from the Norris Building. 15 series. PDF finding aid available.

- **Sub-series 12B: Kamp Kanawana** (ALREADY TRACKED as `src_concordia_atom_kanawana`) -- [AtoM page](https://concordia.accesstomemory.org/kamp-kanawana). 7.72m of textual records, photographs, objects, maps, drawings, audio, film. Dates 1900s-2003. Subdivided into 7 sub-sub-series:
  - 12B01: General administration (directors reports 1910-1969)
  - 12B02: Financial administration
  - 12B03: Land/facilities/equipment/supplies (blueprints, maps)
  - 12B04: Communications (Green Triangle 1932-1940, KA-News 1976-1982, brochures 1921-2001, radio broadcasts 1936-1941)
  - 12B05: Staff/counsellors (Dimock research studies 1960-1961)
  - 12B06: Campers (applications, rosters)
  - 12B07: Program (ceremonies, canoe trip logs, song books)

- **Sub-series 12A: Camping Committees** (ALREADY TRACKED as `src_concordia_atom_12A`) -- Key documents: Ralph Dawson "History of Kamp Kanawana" (1933), R.L. Charlton notes (1943), W.E. Cushing sketches (1943), Permanent Camp Committee minutes (1895-1896), 1895 Summer Camp journal.

- **Full camping series (12A-12N)** documents all YMCA Montreal camps: Kanawana, Otoreke, Becsies, Dorval, Weredale, Perrot, Ski lodge, Thunderbird, Wilderness Survival, Voyageurs de la Verendrye, Lac St-Joseph/Camp Jubilee, Day camp, Camping associations.

#### Concordia Sub-series 5E05

- **Planning and Development** -- Kamp Kanawana report on Planning and Development (1967), Planning and Development Camping Commission records (1968-1970).

#### Concordia Sub-series 12N03

- **Camping Associations** -- Correspondence regarding founding of Canadian Camping Association (1936-1937), Constitution (1937, 1942), Executive meetings (1937-1942), Camping school at McGill University (1937-1940), Lists of camps/officials/counsellors (1939-1948), Quebec Camping Association standards (1970).

#### McMorris Thesis (Spectrum Repository)

- (ALREADY TRACKED as `src_mcmorris_thesis`) -- Grace McMorris, *An Experience That Lasts a Lifetime: Building Modernity, Man, and Nation at the YMCA of Montreal's Kamp Kanawana, 1894-1967*. MA thesis, Concordia, August 2023. Supervisor: Peter Gossage. [Full PDF available](https://spectrum.library.concordia.ca/id/eprint/992763/1/McMorris_MA_F2023.pdf).

#### Promotional Film

- **"Kamp Kanawana: The Experience that lasts a lifetime"** (ALREADY TRACKED via `src_concordia_atom_12B04`) -- [AtoM entry](https://concordia.accesstomemory.org/index.php/kamp-kanawana-the-experience-that-lasts-a-lifetime). ### 5. Academic Sources

#### Grace McMorris Thesis (2023)

- (ALREADY TRACKED) -- The primary academic work on Kamp Kanawana. Examines religion, colonialism, and national identity at the camp from 1894-1967. From 1894 to 1967, Kanawana was open only to boys. Available on [CORE](https://core.ac.uk/outputs/591919397/) and [Spectrum](https://spectrum.library.concordia.ca/id/eprint/992763/).

#### Hedley G. Dimock Research (1960s)

- (ALREADY TRACKED via `src_concordia_atom_12B05`) -- Multiple studies conducted at Kanawana:
  - "A Study of the Change in Attitudes and Temperament of Counselors during a Summer in Camp" (1961)
  - "A Study of the Prediction of Success of Twenty-Seven Camp Counselors" (1961)
  - "Selecting and Training Group Leaders" Project Report #10 (1967) and #13 (1969)
  - "Research on the Selection and Training of Camp Leaders" (1970)

### 6. St-Adolphe-d'Howard / Laurentians Local History

#### QAHN (Quebec Anglophone Heritage Network)

- **"The YMCA Camp of Saint-Adolphe d'Howard"** (ALREADY TRACKED as `src_qahn_howard`) -- [Full article](https://qahn.org/article/ymca-camp-saint-adolphe-dhoward). Key facts:
  - 1892: Lemuel Cushing brought boys to Lake Saint-Joseph in Saint-Adolphe-d'Howard
  - 1893/1894: Camp Jubilee opened (named after YMCA's 50th anniversary)
  - Local entrepreneur Victor Bergeron built a two-story log cabin for 25 young men
  - Camp Otoreke ("Iroquoian for north") succeeded Camp Jubilee on the same site
  - First year: 80 campers over 7 weeks at $34/person including room and board

- **Camp Otoreke photograph, 1944** -- [QAHN image](https://qahn.org/image/camp-otoreke-ymca-camp-saint-adolph-dhoward-1944). **Not yet in sources.json.** Historical photograph of Camp Otoreke (YMCA Camp) at Saint-Adolphe-d'Howard.

#### BaladoDecouverte

- **Saint-Adolphe-d'Howard History** (NEW) -- [Heritage walking tour](https://baladodecouverte.com/circuits/900/poi/10164/saint-adolphe-d-howard-history). The municipality changed its name from Howard Township to Saint-Adolphe-d'Howard in 1939. Before the railway, life was marked by poverty. The area became a recreational destination, with Villa Howard earning the reputation "Miami of the Laurentians" in the 1950s. **Not yet in sources.json.**

#### Lac Kanawana

- **Physical characteristics** (from [Conseil regional de l'environnement des Laurentides](https://crelaurentides.org/lake/kanawana/)): Surface area 0.235 km2, max depth 12.5m, mean depth 5.4m, altitude 318.4m, watershed 5.17 km2, water renewal time 0.43 years. Located in Saint-Sauveur, MRC Les Pays-d'en-Haut.

- **Wikidata entry** for Lac Kanawana: [Q22660425](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q22660425).

- Three private lakes at the camp: Lake Kanawana, Lake Wilson, and Round Lake.

#### Left Coast Mumblings Blog

Personal account connecting YMCA Camp Lewis, Weredale House, and the Montreal YMCA community. Frank Crabbe was an ex-Weredale Boy who died in Vietnam in 1966.
