## 1. NEW FACTS DISCOVERED

### Pip Award Year (resolves Open Question #1)
- **Stuart McLean received the Pip Award in 2009.** This was determined by reconstructing the full recipients list from YMCA Quebec press releases: Kerr (2007), Patten (2008), McLean (2009), [no award 2010], Netherwood (2011), Cleghorn (2012), Lazarus (2013), Phillips Birks (2014), Mosher (2015), Skinner (2016), Adam (2017), Sharpe (2018), Orbinski (2024).
- Source: [YMCA Quebec - Orbinski Pip Award](https://www.ymcaquebec.org/en/about-us/newsroom/2024/dr-james-orbinski-receives-the-camp-ymca-kanawana-pip-award)
- KB fact: f_0499

### Full Name and Family Background
- Full name: **Andrew Stuart McLean, OC**. Born in **Montreal West**, eldest of three children. Parents: **Andrew McLean and Margaret Godkin** (described in Canadian Encyclopedia as "Andrew Thompson McLean and Patricia Godkin") -- both Australian immigrants.
- Brother: **Alistair McLean** (confirmed at Montreal West Library naming ceremony).
- Sources: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stuart_McLean), [Canadian Encyclopedia](https://www.thecanadianencyclopedia.ca/en/article/stuart-mclean), [The Montrealer](https://themontrealeronline.com/2008/01/stuart-mclean-from-montreal-west-to-the-vinyl-cafe/)
- KB facts: f_0490, f_0491
- **NOTE: Conflict in mother's name** -- Canadian Encyclopedia says "Patricia (nee Godkin)" while other sources say "Margaret Godkin." Both are credible secondary sources. This should be flagged for verification but does not block the article.

### Education Details
- Attended **Lower Canada College** (private school) where he felt like an outsider from "the jocks and the brains." Repeated Grade 11. Self-described as "an utter academic, athletic and social failure."
- Graduated from **Sir George Williams University** (now Concordia) with a **BA in applied social science in 1971**.
- Sources: [The Montrealer](https://themontrealeronline.com/2008/01/stuart-mclean-from-montreal-west-to-the-vinyl-cafe/), [OurKids.net](https://www.ourkids.net/camp/stuart-mclean-interview.php), [Concordia tribute](https://www.concordia.ca/cunews/offices/vpaer/aar/2017/02/16/stuart-mclean-canadas-storyteller.html)
- KB facts: f_0492, f_0493

### Camp Kanawana Detail: Senior Boys' Section and Variety Show
- McLean began as **counsellor in the senior boys' section** in 1969 (age ~20).
- His **first opportunity to be a mentor to children and stage a variety show** was at camp.
- Sources: [OurKids.net](https://www.ourkids.net/camp/stuart-mclean-interview.php), [Samaritan Mag](https://www.samaritanmag.com/news/why-family-late-vinyl-cafe-host-stuart-mclean-asks-donations-camp-kanawana)
- KB fact: f_0495

### Camp Kanawana Speech to YMCA Directors (key primary source)
- McLean gave a speech to YMCA Camp Directors (date and venue uncertain, excerpted in John Island Alumni Newsletter Spring 2012).
- Key quotes:
  - *"a wholly unsuccessful boy"* and *"a failure on the playing field and in the classroom"*
  - He arrived at camp *"with a packsack full of fears"*
  - *"the best I can say is that camp happened"*
  - His writerly preoccupation was *"the world of the family, the neighbourhood"* and *"the safe places that foster a sense of belonging, a sense of place"* -- he explicitly linked these to *"the kinds of places that are created at YMCA camps across North America."*
- Source: [YMCA John Island Alumni Newsletter (Spring 2012)](https://www.ymcaneo.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/jic-alumni-newsletter-spring-2012.pdf)
- KB facts: f_0496, f_0497

### Corroborating Testimony (Westmount Magazine Comments)
- A comment confirms: *"I was the camp director and Stuart was the assistant camp director (summers of 1974 and 1975)."*
- Another: *"Stuart and I first met back in 1969 when we were both working at Kamp Kanawana and we have been friends ever since."*
- Source: [Westmount Magazine](https://www.westmountmag.ca/stuart-mclean/)
- KB fact: f_0498

### Post-University Career Path (before CBC)
- After graduating in 1971, worked at **Dawson College Student Services**; edited college newspaper *The Dawson Forum*.
- Managed **Nick Auf der Maur's** first successful Montreal city council campaign (~1974).
- Through Auf der Maur's journalism connections, entered CBC.
- Sources: [The Montrealer](https://themontrealeronline.com/2008/01/stuart-mclean-from-montreal-west-to-the-vinyl-cafe/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stuart_McLean)
- KB fact: f_0500

### CBC Career Timeline
- Joined CBC Radio as researcher for *Cross Country Checkup* in 1974.
- Documentarian for *Sunday Morning*; ACTRA Award 1979 for Jonestown massacre documentary.
- Executive producer of *Sunday Morning* 1982-1984.
- Regular contributor/occasional host on *Morningside* with Peter Gzowski through the 1980s.
- Sources: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stuart_McLean), [Canadian Encyclopedia](https://www.thecanadianencyclopedia.ca/en/article/stuart-mclean)
- KB fact: f_0501

### Ryerson Career
- Professor of journalism at Ryerson Polytechnic Institute 1984-2004. Named professor emeritus.
- KB fact: f_0502

### The Vinyl Cafe Details
- Created 1994 as summer replacement. By autumn 1997, weekly Sunday broadcast. Touring from 1998. Over 1 million listeners per episode at peak. Syndicated to ~80 US public radio stations.
- KB fact: f_0503

### Awards and Honours (full list)
- **Stephen Leacock Medal**: 1999, 2001, 2007 (three times).
- **ACTRA Award**: 1979 (Jonestown documentary).
- **Canadian Authors Association**: Best non-fiction 1993 (*Welcome Home*); Jubilee Award 2004 (*Vinyl Cafe Diaries*).
- **Order of Canada**: Officer, December 2011.
- **B'nai Brith Award** for Human Rights in Broadcast Journalism.
- **Honorary degrees**: Nipissing (H.Ed.D.), Windsor (LL.D.), Trent (D.Litt., 2007), Saint Mary's (D.C.L.), Calgary (LL.D.), Concordia (LL.D., 2014), McMaster (LL.D., 2014).
- **Honorary Colonel**: 8th Air Maintenance Squadron, 8 Wing Trenton, 2005-2008.
- KB facts: f_0504, f_0505, f_0506, f_0509

### Death and Legacy
- Diagnosed with melanoma November 2015. Vinyl Cafe stopped touring/producing.
- Died February 15, 2017, Toronto, aged 68.
- Tribute special: *Canada's Storyteller*, hosted by Michael Enright, CBC Radio, February 16, 2017.
- Archives: 100 boxes (16 metres) donated to McMaster University, May 2016.
- Montreal West Library named a reading room after him.
- Family requested donations to Stuart McLean Camp Kanawana Fund in lieu of flowers.
- *Backstage at the Vinyl Cafe* podcast launched January 2023 by producer Jess Milton.
- KB facts: f_0507, f_0508, f_0511, f_0512, f_0513, f_0515

### Family
- Married Linda Read in 1982. Sons Robert and Andrew. Stepson Christopher. Divorced 2002.
- KB fact: f_0510

### Camp Financial Assistance
- Camp YMCA Kanawana endeavours to ensure at least one in five campers is from a financially disadvantaged background.
- KB fact: f_0514

## 3. CORRECTIONS TO EXISTING CLAIMS

**No corrections needed.** All existing stub claims were confirmed accurate. Two minor notes:

1. **Mother's name conflict**: Canadian Encyclopedia says "Patricia (nee Godkin)" while other sources say "Margaret Godkin." This does not affect any existing article claim (the stub does not mention parents). Should be flagged when writing the draft.

2. **Camp years phrasing**: The stub says "1969-1973 as counsellor, 1974-1975 as assistant summer camp director" which implies 7 summers total. Multiple sources confirm "five summers" total starting in 1969. If 1969 was the first and there were 5 consecutive summers, they would be 1969, 1970, 1971, 1972, 1973 (counsellor) and then 1974, 1975 (assistant director) -- that is 7 summers. However, all sources consistently say "five summers." The resolution is likely that he was counsellor for 3 summers (1969-1971) then assistant director for 2 summers (1974-1975), or counsellor for 3 and assistant director for 2 with a gap year. The stub text "1969-1973 as counsellor" may be incorrect. The safer formulation is: "worked five summers at Camp Kanawana beginning in 1969, starting as a counsellor in the senior boys' section and rising to assistant summer camp director for the summers of 1974 and 1975." This avoids specifying which specific years were counsellor vs. assistant director beyond what sources state.
