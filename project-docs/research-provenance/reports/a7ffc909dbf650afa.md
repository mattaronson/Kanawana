### Key Findings

1. **Lac des Becs-scie is NOT one of Kanawana's three private lakes.** It is a separate, adjacent lake ~1.5 km to the west, now fully residential with ~240 homes. Kanawana's three lakes remain Lake Kanawana, Lake Wilson, and Round Lake.

2. **Location confirmed**: Saint-Sauveur (83%) and Mille-Isles (17%), coordinates ~45deg51'12"N, 74deg12'53"W, altitude 320.5m ASL. The lake is crescent-shaped, 0.5 km sq (actually larger than Lac Kanawana at 0.235 km sq), 2.2 km long, max depth 16.6m.

3. **Camp Becsies was a separate YMCA Montreal camp** on this lake, with its own archival sub-series (P0145/12D). It operated 1929-1936 (including hosting Montreal Protestant Orphans' Home campers) and 1960-1971 (family camping). A 24-year gap in the record suggests possible inactivity.

4. **Etymology**: "Bec-scie" = Quebec French for merganser (sawbill duck), from 17th-century New France usage.

5. **1938 Green Triangle connection**: A limerick mentions Kanawana staff member Wallace going to "Leke Becsies," confirming staff overlap between the two adjacent camps.

6. **The lake was divested after 1971** and subsequently developed as a residential area. A chapel (Sainte-Therese-du-Lac-des-Becs-Scies) was built on the lake in 1950-1951.
