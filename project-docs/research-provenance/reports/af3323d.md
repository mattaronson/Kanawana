# Research Report: Camp Kanawana, Shawbridge, and Regional YMCA History

## Search 1: "Shawbridge" Quebec History YMCA Boys Farm

**Findings:** Rich results. The Shawbridge Boys' Farm and Training School was conceived in 1899 by James R. Dick (superintendent of the Boys' Home of Montreal) and built in 1907 on 250 acres of farmland in the Laurentian foothills, approximately 40 miles north of Montreal. It opened in spring 1909 receiving boys from Sherbrooke Jail and the Roman Catholic Reformatory. Its founding president was John Dougall, publisher of the Montreal Witness. The YMCA connection surfaces through Wayne Hanna, who studied at George Williams College in Chicago (a YMCA training centre) before joining Shawbridge to transform the custodial program into a treatment program. The institution's geographic proximity to Kamp Kanawana (both in the Laurentians north of Montreal) is notable.

## Search 11: "Pine Crest" YMCA Camp Ontario "Lumbermen" "Voyageurs"

**Findings:** Extremely strong results. YMCA Camp Pine Crest is located on Clear Lake near Torrance, Muskoka Lakes, Ontario, operating continuously since 1910. It originated as the Broadview Boys Institute (founded 1896 by C.J. Atkinson). The camp's signature tradition is the **Pine Crest Games**, a two-day all-camp competition held at the end of every summer since 1940, dividing the camp into two teams: **Lumbermen** (symbol: axe) and **Voyageurs** (symbol: paddle). Team leaders must hold their symbol without letting it touch the ground for two days. Competitions span Swimming, Boating, and Woodcraft (portaging, tug-of-war, giant jenga). The rules for selecting leaders and determining winners were made a sworn secret in the 1970s; those who learn the secrets become "Referees-in-Chief" (RICs) and can never compete again. A book commemorating the camp's centenary, *Lumbermen & Voyageurs: The YMCA Pine Crest Story*, is available for $19.00. Camp property is nearly 650 acres with four miles of shoreline.

**Key URLs:**
- [Lumbermen & Voyageurs: The YMCA Pine Crest Story -- Shop](https://camp-pine-crest.myshopify.com/products/lumbermen-voyageurs-the-ymca-pine-crest-story)
