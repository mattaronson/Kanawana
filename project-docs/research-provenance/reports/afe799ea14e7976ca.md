## Research Report: Canadian Camping Magazine on Archive.org

### Are Issues Available?

**Yes.** Digitized issues of *Canadian Camping* magazine (official publication of the Canadian Camping Association, 1949-1988) are hosted on the Internet Archive. They were uploaded by user "yoyojoe" (likely Joe Richards, an Ontario camp professional) and belong to the **canadian-camping-association** collection. Issues were scanned at 600 PPI with ABBYY OCR, meaning full-text search should be possible within archive.org itself.

### Related Discovery: CCA Conference in Quebec (1974)

An ERIC document on archive.org contains the **Proceedings of the Canadian Camping Association Outdoor Education Conference**, held at **La Caleche, Ste-Agathe, Quebec, October 3-6, 1974** -- in the Laurentians, near Kanawana. This could contain relevant material.
- URL: `https://archive.org/details/ERIC_ED106010`

### Trent University Archives (Physical Holdings)

Trent University Archives holds a **complete physical run** of *Canadian Camping* magazine as part of the Canadian Camping Association fonds. They confirm that digitized copies are also available via the Internet Archive. Related accessions:
- [CCA fonds (original accession 78-004)](https://archives.trentu.ca/index.php/78-004)
- [CCA fonds (2008 additions)](https://archives.trentu.ca/index.php/08-010)
- [Guide to Camp Collections](https://www.trentu.ca/library/archives-special-collections/archival-research-guides/guide-camp-collections)

### Concordia YMCA Fonds Connection

The Concordia University Archives hold the YMCA of Montreal fonds, which references **Canadian Camping Association reports on conducting camping and outdoor education programs** in connection with YMCA camp operations in Quebec -- directly linking the CCA and its publications to the Kanawana orbit.
- [YMCA of Montreal fonds](https://www.concordia.ca/offices/archives/ymca-fonds-sub-series-12A.html)
