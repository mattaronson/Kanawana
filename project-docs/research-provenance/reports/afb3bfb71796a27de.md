### 2. Fonds 72-007: OCA Original Accession

- **Dates:** 1922-1973
- **Extent:** 3.2 metres of textual records, ~600 photographs
- **Creator:** Ontario Camping Association (founded 1933 by Taylor Statten; renamed Ontario Camps Association in 2012)
- **Series 1 -- Camp Brochures and Photographs (1922-1967):** Alphabetical by camp name. Appendix lists photographs.
- **Series 2 -- Organizational Records (1952-1973):** Camp Staff Records, Advisory Board, Archives, History, OCA Board, Conference Committee, Education Committee, Executive Committee, Nominating Committee, Canoeing Standards Committee, Publications, Speeches, Conference Records.

**Kanawana reference:** A file labelled **"Kanawana (1940)"** exists in the Camp Brochures and Photographs series, filed alphabetically between Kagawong and Kandalore. This likely contains a Kanawana camp brochure dated circa 1940.

### 3. Fonds 78-006: OCA 1978 Additions

- **Dates:** 1939-1978
- **Series A** -- Board, Executive, Correspondence and Office Files (1938-1978): 6 subseries including Board/Executive Minutes, Executive Files, Reading Files, Subject Correspondence, Office Files, Counsellor Placement Records
- **Series B** -- Financial Records (1948-1969)
- **Series C** -- Committees (1949-1977): Archives, Conference, Constitution committees
- **Series D** -- Agency Liaison (1949-1978): CCA, Provincial Associations, ACA, Other Agencies

**Kanawana reference:** In the **Agency Liaison series (Series D)**, Kanawana is listed among **"Y" camps** alongside: Wanakita, Wa-sa-ah-bun, Tapatoo, Tapawingo, Pinecrest, and Camp Stephens.

### 4. Related Fonds at Trent
