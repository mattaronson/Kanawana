## Research Report: Edgar E. Smee (c. 1912/1913 -- 2002)

### Biographical Overview

Edgar E. "Ed" Smee died peacefully on **February 7, 2002**, at the **Glebe Centre in Ottawa**, at age **89**. This places his birth at approximately **1912 or 1913** (the obituary says 89 in 2002). He was a native of **Hamilton, Ontario**, where he grew up and swam as a boy at **Red Hill Creek**.

**Family:** Wife **Frieda**; four daughters: **Linda, Susan, Sydney, and Sonia**; brothers **Norman** and **Leonard**. His obituary was published in **The Globe and Mail** on February 11, 2002. A memorial gathering was held **February 16, 2002** at the **Workers Arts and Heritage Centre, 51 Stuart Street, Hamilton**.

### The 30-Year Absence and Return (c. 1938--1968)

The EOY Awards biography states that Ed Smee returned "after a 30-year absence" to his hometown of Hamilton **in 1968**. This means he left Hamilton around **1938** -- which aligns precisely with the Kanawana camp director period (1939/1942). He likely left Hamilton for Montreal or elsewhere in his mid-twenties for YMCA work, and his 30-year absence would encompass his entire career away from Hamilton. No online sources mention his YMCA or camp connections; that period falls squarely within the undocumented "absent" years.

### Key Environmental Achievements

1. **Founding Chair of the Conserver Society of Hamilton & District** -- the organization that grew out of CHOP and has been active for over 50 years.
2. **Helped create the first action plan for Hamilton Harbour/Burlington Bay** -- credited by his colleagues at the Conserver Society.
3. **Initiated the Environmental Trust Fund (1986)** that continues as the Ed Smee Fund, providing grants for environmental education, research, and advocacy in Hamilton and Burlington.
4. The Conserver Society's work under his leadership included: composting programs, bicycle paths, pesticide reduction, limiting urban sprawl, waste management, air quality, and transportation initiatives.

### The Ed Smee Fund (Legacy)

The **Ed Smee Conserver Society Environmental Fund** is managed by the **Hamilton Community Foundation**. The Conserver Society serves as advisor and recommends grant recipients. It funds small community projects focused on environmental education, research, or advocacy in Hamilton and Burlington. Donations are tax-deductible. The fund continues to operate as of 2026.

### The Environmentalist of the Year Award

The award program Ed Smee helped build (and won in 1983) has continued since 1979. It is now co-sponsored by the Conserver Society, Hamilton Naturalists' Club, Hamilton Conservation Authority, Environment Hamilton, Bay Area Restoration Council, and others. The award is named the **Dr. Victor Cecilioni Award** after its 1979 inaugural recipient.
