## A: ANALYZE -- Key Discoveries

### 1. MAJOR DISCOVERY: Ross & Macdonald Designed a Doctor's Cottage for Kamp Kanawana

The **Canadian Centre for Architecture (CCA)** holds architectural drawings in the Ross & Macdonald fonds for a **"Doctor's Cottage for Kamp Kanawana"** (URL: `https://www.cca.qc.ca/en/archives/45474/fonds-ross-macdonald/387039/projects/50576/doctors-cottage-for-kamp-kanawana`).

This is significant because:
- **Ross & Macdonald** was one of Canada's most prominent architectural firms, responsible for the Chateau Laurier, Royal York Hotel, Mount Royal Hotel (1920-22), Maple Leaf Gardens, and many other landmarks.
- The firm designed **"several buildings for the YMCA in Montreal (1909-13)"**, placing the Doctor's Cottage likely in that period.
- This is the **first professionally designed building** identified for the Kanawana site.
- The "Doctor's Cottage" may be related to the **1922 Hospital** documented in the brochures, or it could be an earlier or different structure. The exact date and whether it was built are not confirmed from online sources alone.
- The CCA page could not be fetched (403), so the exact date and drawing details remain unknown.

**Confidence:** High (CCA archival record). **New fact:** f_0573, f_0574.

### 2. MAJOR DISCOVERY: Clivus Multrum Composting Toilet Infrastructure

Camp Kanawana is listed as a project in the **Clivus Multrum** (composting toilet manufacturer) portfolio, with the following specifications:
- **16 commercial composters** accommodating 22,500 uses each per year
- **32 dry toilets** saving **576,000 gallons of water** each year
- **2 central buildings** containing all bathroom and shower facilities
- The system **eliminated the old septic system** to keep Lake Kanawana safe from pollution
- Kanawana is described as a "year-round environmental education facility and summer camp"

This is entirely new infrastructure information not in the article. The installation date is not stated. The YMCA website also mentions "eco-friendly bathroom and shower facilities" and "16 commercial composters," confirming these details.

**Confidence:** High (manufacturer's project portfolio + YMCA website). **New fact:** f_0572, f_0582.

### 3. SIGNIFICANT: McConnell Foundation $700,000 for Renovations (2023-2027)

The **McConnell Foundation** (a major Montreal-based philanthropy founded 1937) provided **$700,000** to the YMCAs of Quebec for **major renovations at Camp Kanawana**, covering 2023-2027. This is part of the same **YMCA Springboard for Life** campaign that received the 2018 Desjardins $1M donation.

The Foundation's statement: "The McConnell Foundation is proud to have contributed to Camp Kanawana over the years. This iconic place continues to inspire generations of young people."

The specifics of what renovations are planned or completed are **not documented publicly**.

An Indeed.com posting also mentions that "for the past year, our facilities team has been working hard to carry out much needed renovations to the camp" -- suggesting active renovation work, likely post-2023.

**Confidence:** High. **New fact:** f_0575.

### 4. SIGNIFICANT: Desjardins Pavilion -- Still Unconfirmed

Despite targeted searching, **no evidence was found that the Desjardins Pavilion was actually constructed**. The 2018 La Presse announcement described a "$1 million contribution" with a portion for "a new community pavilion -- the 'new heart of Kanawana.'" But:
- No subsequent reporting on groundbreaking, construction, or completion
- The current YMCA facilities page does not list a "Desjardins Pavilion" by name
- The "Pavilion for Workshops and Training" listed on the facilities page may or may not be this building
- GLCRM Architectes and Garoy Construction are YMCA Foundation major partners, suggesting they may be involved in design/construction

**Status:** The article's current cautious language ("Whether this pavilion was constructed as announced has not been confirmed") remains appropriate.

### 5. SIGNIFICANT: Archival Records Pinpointed

Two key archival collections have been more precisely identified:

**Concordia University Archives:**
- **P0145/12B03** -- "Land, facilities, equipment, supplies" sub-series, specifically about Kanawana property and buildings
- **1946 "Layout, Building Designs, and Equipment"** document in the Committees sub-series (P0145/12A)
- Blueprints and maps are confirmed among the materials

**Canadian Centre for Architecture:**
- Ross & Macdonald fonds holds the Doctor's Cottage drawings

These are the two locations where construction dates and building histories would be documented.

**New facts:** f_0578, f_0579.

### 6. USEFUL: Detailed Accommodation Specifications

Previously undocumented details about accommodations:
- Junior cabins: 10 capacity (6 upper bunks + 4 lower bunks), 2 staff in separate room, cubbies for each camper
- Senior platform tents: wooden platform + heavy canvas tent + waterproof tarp, 8 campers + 1-2 staff, shared space
- Section gender assignments: Pathfinders/Pioneers = girls + non-binary; Coureurs des Bois/Woodsmen = boys + non-binary; Senior Kanawanians/Voyageurs = co-ed
- Rose des Vents: 16-person max, 3 rooms, wood-burning stove
- Dining Hall: industrial kitchen (not available to rental groups)

**New facts:** f_0585, f_0586, f_0587, f_0588, f_0589.

### 7. USEFUL: Kanawana Adventure Day Camp (2024)

A new program launched in summer 2024, indicating continued facility investment:
- "Kanawana Adventure" / "Aventure a Kanawana"
- Day camp for ages 5-12, in English
- Uses the overnight camp site in Saint-Sauveur
- Director: **Marie-Pierre Lacasse**
- Covered by Nouvelles Laurentides local news

**New fact:** f_0580.

### 8. USEFUL: Property Size Discrepancy

Two figures are in circulation:
- **550 acres** (YMCA official website, current)
- **455 acres** (Wikimapia, older third-party source)

The 95-acre discrepancy likely reflects land acquisition over time. The YMCA website is authoritative for the current figure.

**New fact:** f_0581.
