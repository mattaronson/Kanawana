### CONCLUSION

The project's mining of the Concordia Archives is best described as **deep catalog knowledge, zero primary document access**. The finding aid has been thoroughly extracted and the catalog structure is well understood. But the actual archival materials -- the committee minutes, season reports, scrapbooks, ceremony scripts, song books, canoe trip logs, photographs, maps, and blueprints -- have never been read. The 70 "Concordia facts" in the KB are facts *about what the archives contain*, not facts *from the archival documents themselves*.

The 9 items accessed via Internet Archive represent the only actual content from these archives that has been read, and they cover a tiny fraction of the holdings (estimated at less than 1% of the 7.72 metres of textual records).

A physical visit to the Concordia University Records Management & Archives Special Collections Reading Room would unlock the single largest untapped source of primary data for this project. The fonds-level PDF finding aid (`concordia.accesstomemory.org/downloads/ymca-of-montreal-fonds-2.pdf`) should also be obtained, as the current finding aid extraction was done via search engine indexing and is acknowledged as incomplete for sub-series 12B02, 12B03, and 12B06.
