# Canadian Camping — Catalogue of Articles 1949–1963

Transcribed verbatim (OCR, uncorrected) from *Canadian Camping* Vol. 16 No. 1,
December 1963, pp. 15–22: "CATALOGUE OF ARTICLES appearing in CANADIAN CAMPING
1949 — 1963, Compiled by W. E. Yard."

This is the magazine's own cumulative subject index for its first fifteen years,
classified under sixteen topics. It is kept here as a finding aid and as a
**checklist**: every article listed for an issue this pass has read should appear
in that issue's block in `cc_findings.md`, and any that does not is a hole to be
patched. Dates and spellings are the index's own — it contains OCR noise and at
least one plain error ("Hang On To Your Teen-Agers—Barry Powers" is Barry Lowes;
"Le Camp-Ecole Trois-Saumons" is filed under Dec. 1962, correctly). Note also
that the index runs only to June 1963, so the December 1963 issue that carries it
is not in it.

**A series worth reading as a set, whether or not the individual blocks caught it.**
W. E. Yard's **"Who Dozit?"** ran through the mid-1950s as a set of camp job
descriptions, and the catalogue lists at least nine: Section Director (Dec 1954),
Business Manager (Jun 1955), Waterfront Director (Jun 1955), Out-Trip Director (Jun
1955), Medical Staff (Jun 1955), Camp Program (Jun 1956), Evaluation (Dec 1956),
Counsellors' Responsibilities (Apr 1957), Swim Test and Recognition (Jun 1957).
Together they are the era's fullest statement of what each role in a Canadian camp
was actually for — the best available frame for reading Kanawana's own staff lists,
where the wiki has titles but often no duties. Worth collecting into one place at
conversion time.
